Databricks tests context-aware data agents on 401 workplace tasks

Databricks compared Genie Code with three general-purpose coding agents on 401 tasks derived from internal use. Each agent received its native harness, access to Databricks through MCP, and a 20-minute budget. The company reports that Genie Code reached 76.6% accuracy at a mean cost of $0.55 per task, attributing its performance to semantic search, persistent memory, and workspace context.

Immune-receptor benchmark uncovers a germline shortcut in protein language models

Across B-cell and T-cell receptor retrieval datasets, classical sequence alignment consistently beat frozen protein-language-model embeddings on antigen-specific CDR3 sequences. The models approached parity only when conserved framework regions were included, while germline-reversion experiments suggested that much of the improvement came from germline-derived context rather than better extraction of antigen-specific signals.

The study also found that random data splits inflated retrieval accuracy by roughly 15–28 percentage points through clonal leakage, reinforcing the case for clone-aware evaluation.

CAD-guided diffusion makes global protein shape programmable

A blueprint-guided RFdiffusion framework uses signed-distance and point-cloud objectives to constrain generated protein backbones to user-specified three-dimensional envelopes. Experimental work produced stable single-chain shapes, a rare left-handed β-sheet architecture, and tunable rings and helical filaments.

The researchers also coupled a designed helical propeller to an F1-ATPase rotor and observed ATP-dependent motion, although they did not establish directional swimming.

DECAF designs molecules around conformational ensembles

Decoupled Annealing Flows separates molecular generation into a graph-conditioned conformational-ensemble model and a coordinate-conditioned molecular-graph model. Its annealing loop evaluates proposed molecules using ensemble statistics, reducing the danger of optimizing an unusually favorable single conformer that does not represent the molecule’s Boltzmann distribution.

Demonstrations included tuning mean radius of gyration, solvent-accessible area, multi-objective trade-offs, and higher moments such as conformational variance and skewness. Selected results were checked with all-atom molecular dynamics.
