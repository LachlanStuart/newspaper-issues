Biology & genomics

sctRNA-seq brings transfer RNAs into multimodal single-cell analysis

sctRNA-seq adds scalable tRNA and mRNA co-quantification to a widely adopted single-cell platform. The reported method produces thousands of tRNA unique molecular identifiers per cell and supports additional protein and guide-RNA measurements. That combination makes it possible to examine variation in tRNA pools alongside cell state and genomic perturbations.

Research announcement

WALRUS tackles alignment-driven false positives in evolutionary inference

WALRUS integrates phylogeny-aware codon alignment, tree/network inference and eight selection tests into a coordinate-consistent HyPhy 3 workflow. It models alignment uncertainty, base-composition drift and clustered artifacts rather than treating them as evidence of selection.

Biology+AI Daily reports that, across 800 indel simulations, the method recovered 94.6% of true alignment columns. Indel-driven BUSTED false positives fell from 44.7–58.1% with common aligners to 4.4%, or 1.4% with BUSTED-E. Tests on 11,190 avian orthogroups reportedly ran 6.6–35.8 times faster than separate selection tools.

WALRUS report

Genomic tessellations distinguish sequence components from their arrangement

A genomic-tessellation framework represents DNA by separating its constituent components from their geometry. Accompanying posts describe self-tessellation as a way to expose homology obscured by array-length variation and local divergence. A compressed tessellated Burrows–Wheeler transform supports exact-match queries while preserving access to geometric differences.

GENESIS follow-up favors more conditions over more cells per condition

A GENESIS follow-up reports that predictions of drug- and cytokine-response distributions approached the reproducibility of replicate experiments. At a fixed total number of training cells, expanding the range of experimental conditions improved predictions more than collecting additional cells per condition—a practical finding for allocating experimental effort.

PDBe update connects structures, literature and simulations

The PDBe 2027 update adds residue-level literature annotations, molecular-dynamics datasets and simulation-derived properties. Reported literature coverage spans 120,569 PDB entries, and MDposit links cover approximately 187,503 protein simulations.

Other additions include membrane-contact annotations, standardized channel and tunnel analyses, and three MCP interfaces for REST APIs, search and knowledge-graph exploration. Literature annotations for another 32,427 structures are withheld because of licensing restrictions.

Protein models & medicine

Germinal Multi designs antibodies across EGFR resistance variants

Germinal Multi optimizes a shared antibody sequence against multiple antigen states, supporting both de novo design and redesign of existing antibodies. In the reported cancer application, four of 60 screened designs exceeded a strong binding-response threshold against wild-type EGFR and all six tested resistance variants.

The study also reports antiviral antibody experiments. Its key qualification is that broad binding does not necessarily mean functional neutralization, underscoring the importance of paired binding and functional assays.

Antibody-design report

Dynamics supervision improves variant-effect prediction, especially for stability

DynamicsPLM adds residue-level and pairwise dynamics supervision to ESM2-35M while retaining masked-language modeling; inference requires sequence alone. Biology+AI Daily reports a mean ProteinGym Spearman correlation of 0.398, compared with 0.330 for the same-backbone ESM2 baseline.

The largest improvements were in stability assays. Binding-assay separation was weaker, and combining evolutionary and dynamics scores could underperform dynamics-only scoring for viral proteins.

DynamicsPLM report

NMR follow-up measures persistent chemical-environment prediction errors

A follow-up NMR study describes rBMRB, a curated resource containing 6,123 chemical-shift entries. The best tested predictors remained near 0.9 ppm median error, against an estimated intrinsic disagreement floor of roughly 0.4–0.5 ppm. More training data and re-referencing brought little improvement.

Difficult sites often combined dense packing with dynamics. The proposed SLAE-ERR error predictor improved filtering of unreliable sites, but alternative conformations and disorder remained important failure sources.

NMR study follow-up

Controlled sequence probes reveal organization inside ESM2

Synthetic polar/nonpolar repeat patterns reveal ESM2 representation axes associated with hydrophobic composition, helix/linker boundaries and repeat phase. The cyclic organization disappears in untrained ESM2 and under arbitrary amino-acid partitions.

ESMFold reproduced structural trends across the design library but did not reliably distinguish successful individual designs. The results separate interpretable representation structure from dependable sequence-level prediction.

Representation study

Electrostatic-field pretraining helps selected recognition tasks

The Biophysical Transformer learns rotationally invariant electrostatic-field descriptors during pretraining, then uses sequence alone at inference. Matched-initialization ablations reportedly improved human thermostability, disorder prediction and two of three interaction tasks.

With the same regression head, its 1.1-million-parameter field-trained encoder outperformed frozen ESM-2 650M on MHC-I and MHC-II binding correlations. Gains were not universal: organism-mixed thermostability showed no improvement with trained heads, and one interaction benchmark remained near chance.

Biophysical Transformer report

OpenMed tests review gates for clinical-note export

OpenMed reports that version 3.3 rejected all ten attempted review bypasses in a synthetic dictation test. Attempts included exporting before drug-name confirmation, reusing an outdated sign-off and writing unreviewed drafts to FHIR. Changing “25 mg” to “25 milligrams” reset two plan statements to unreviewed; only the reviewed note became FHIR R4.

The test used a script acting as reviewer. Drafts remain subject to human review.

Français — Pollution de l’air : malgré des progrès, Paris, Lyon et Marseille encore loin des meilleures villes européennes

Le Monde · 17 novembre 2026 · Lire l’article

Les progrès des grandes villes françaises ne suffisent pas à les placer parmi les bons élèves européens de la qualité de l’air. Le Monde présente le premier classement de Clean Cities, un collectif de plus de 150 organisations non gouvernementales, portant sur 40 métropoles et capitales. Lyon, Paris et Marseille occupent respectivement les 23e, 24e et 25e positions : toutes trois obtiennent un D, dans un système de notation allant de A à F.

La comparaison fait ressortir un contraste géographique marqué. Copenhague, Stockholm et Helsinki arrivent en tête et sont les seules villes à décrocher un A, tandis qu’Athènes et Bucarest reçoivent un F. Cette mise en perspective permet de dépasser le seul constat d’une amélioration locale pour situer les villes françaises par rapport à leurs voisines.

Pourquoi lire : Une lecture courte pour replacer la pollution urbaine française dans un contexte européen et pratiquer le vocabulaire des comparaisons environnementales.

Vocabulaire utile

  • agglomération — ensemble urbain constitué d’une ville et des communes qui forment avec elle une continuité bâtie.
  • zone à faibles émissions — périmètre dans lequel la circulation de certains véhicules polluants est restreinte.
  • lanterne rouge — expression désignant la personne ou l’entité qui occupe la dernière place d’un classement.

日本語 — この島，クリックが止まらない。「Clicky Islands」はドット絵の島を開拓する“放置したくない”インクリメンタルゲームだった【放課後れびゅあーず！】

4Gamer · 2026-11-17 · 記事を読む

ドット絵で描かれた島を、クリックしながら開拓していく「Clicky Islands」を紹介する記事。ポーランドのKarrot Gamesが開発し、ポケットペアが販売を手掛ける作品で、クリック操作による資源集めに、都市建設やリソース管理を組み合わせている。プレイヤーは国王に命じられた総督として未開の島へ渡り、新たな入植地を育てていく。

木を切り、食糧を集め、物資を輸出して島を発展させたら、さらに次の島へ進む。少しずつ成果が積み重なる遊びを、記事は「放置したくない」という切り口で取り上げている。架空の学園を舞台にした連載「放課後れびゅあーず！」の一編で、ゲームの仕組みだけでなく、つい操作を続けたくなる魅力に注目した読み物だ。

読む理由： ポーランド発のゲームを題材に、開拓や資源管理に関する語彙と、日本語のゲーム紹介で使われる表現に触れられる。

語彙メモ

  • インクリメンタルゲーム — 資源や数値を少しずつ増やし、生産力や設備を強化していく種類のゲーム。
  • リソース管理（りそーすかんり） — 食糧や木材など、限られた資源の用途や配分を考えて運用すること。
  • 入植地（にゅうしょくち） — 新たに移り住んだ人々が生活や生産の拠点として築く土地や集落。

中文 — 全球气温持续升高 傅海燕吁合作推动可持续制冷

联合早报 · 2026-11-17 · https://www.zaobao.com.sg/news/singapore/story20261117-10008220

天气越来越热，冷气需求不断增加，如何降温又不加重环境负担？新加坡永续发展与环境部长傅海燕在联合国气候变化大会期间举行的部长级圆桌会议上指出，各国需要共同调动专业知识、资源和资金，加快推广可持续制冷方案。报道指出，全球冷气机数量预计将在2050年增至56亿台；目前已有76个国家支持“全球降温承诺”，目标是在2050年前将制冷相关排放减少68％。

傅海燕提出，落实目标不能只靠单一国家，而要建立政府、城市、企业与合作伙伴之间的合作网络。各国可依据今年9月在新加坡共同制定的行动蓝图，围绕重点方向合作，同时兼顾不同国情。她也建议针对具体议题组建行动联盟，支持各国自主推进重点工作，把国际承诺转化为实际方案。

为什么读： 从新加坡视角了解冷气需求与气候政策之间的关系，也能学习中文环境新闻如何表述国际合作与政策落实。

词汇笔记

  • 可持续制冷 (kě chíxù zhìlěng) — 在满足降温需求的同时，尽量降低能源消耗和环境影响的制冷方式。
  • 制冷相关排放 (zhìlěng xiāngguān páifàng) — 制冷设备运行及相关过程产生的温室气体排放。
  • 行动蓝图 (xíngdòng lántú) — 为实现政策目标而制定的整体行动规划，说明重点方向和推进路径。
