CLAP2 brings variable-length audio encoding to OpenCLIP

Ross Wightman describes CLAP2, or NaFlex CLAP, as using a NaFlexViT audio encoder with log-mel patchification and padding that accommodates different audio lengths per sample. He identifies data curation, scaling and validation as the paper’s central contributions.

The first OpenCLIP 4.x release supports the models and substantially refactors training code. Preliminary scaling experiments ran on the JUPITER supercomputer at Forschungszentrum Jülich.

Clinical extraction: more disease mentions or fewer false alarms?

OpenMed compares mmBERT and GLiNER2.5 on the same 1,723 Spanish disease mentions. mmBERT finds 18 more mentions, while GLiNER2.5 produces 193 fewer false alarms—a concrete tradeoff between recall and false-positive reduction.

The accompanying writeup covers model construction, a sealed test opened once and the errors made by each model.

Genomics & single-cell biology

AlphaGenome 2 adds cell-type and cell-state context

AlphaGenome 2 predicts regulatory activity and variant effects from 1-Mb human or mouse DNA sequences. Condition embeddings modulate sequence representations from the earliest network layers, while separating conditions from output heads enables imputation of unmeasured epigenomic assays.

The approach aligns an expression autoencoder trained on more than 100 million human single-cell profiles with a frozen sequence model. A launch follow-up reports doubled single-cell eQTL recall at high precision, but says the model still misses most variants with high posterior inclusion probabilities.

Shared by razoralign · Research post

scOntoFM builds cell-type hierarchy into representations

Pretrained on 27.5 million human cells, scOntoFM combines masked gene modeling with ontology-dependent contrastive and rank-alignment objectives. Reported evaluations show stronger preservation of cell-type hierarchy while maintaining competitive clustering and annotation performance.

Novel-type identification reaches 28.59% macro-accuracy across 99 unseen types. The report also describes benefits for gene-function prediction and spatial expression imputation.

Biology+AI Daily · Research post

PertVI disentangles treatment effects from baseline state

PertVI separates baseline mitotic phase, cellular state and batch effects from treatment-induced shifts. Abnormal, off-manifold responses occupy a separate anomaly space.

The accompanying discussion reports directional response trajectories and biphasic rebound behavior, arguing that phenotypic shifts depend on state-specific impulses rather than broad transcriptional responses alone.

Shared by razoralign · Research post

CellVocal links transcriptional dynamics to nearby signaling

CellVocal uses velocity-constrained inference to identify communication-mediated transcriptional modulation in spatial transcriptomics. It attributes the inferred communication component to signaling modules and neighboring sender cells. Simulations reportedly recover complex trajectories and communication perturbations.

Shared by razoralign · Research post

LingoCell connects expression measurements with language

LingoCell jointly encodes gene identity and expression magnitude, then aligns those representations with text linking cell identity, disease concepts and tissue environment. It is presented as supporting clustering, batch integration, zero-shot annotation and description generation without fine-tuning, while retaining editable expression levels for perturbation interpretation.

scDeepSim supplies controllable synthetic single-cell data

scDeepSim combines a structured variational autoencoder with latent diffusion to generate single-cell expression profiles. Users can adjust selected biological or technical latent factors at specified strengths, creating synthetic datasets whose intervention parameters provide ground truth for benchmarking.

Joint protein–DNA models guide recognition and editing

PDLMs jointly encode protein and DNA sequences following interaction-aware pretraining on 51,329 experimentally supported pairs. Biology+AI Daily reports improved partner-compatibility classification and binding-effect prediction, experimental improvements in MutH editing, and transfer to TadA activity and off-target prediction.

Fine-tuning also supports transcription-factor motif prediction, including some mutation-driven changes in specificity.

LIMBER combines attention and state-space modeling for RNA

LIMBER is described as a fully open noncoding-RNA foundation model. Its hybrid architecture interleaves bidirectional attention with selective state-space modeling, targeting both ordered sequence dependencies and nonlocal nucleotide interactions for structure and function prediction.

Shared by razoralign · Research post

RPFace exposes bias in RNA–protein interface benchmarks

A leakage-controlled, stratified benchmark finds that co-folding interface accuracy is concentrated in ribosomal complexes and sensitive to experimental modality and evolutionary inputs.

RPFace instead uses sequence-derived representations, RNA secondary-structure probabilities and partner-conditioned attention, without requiring structures or multiple-sequence alignments. Reported post-cutoff AUROCs are approximately 0.88 for protein residues and 0.80 for RNA nucleotides. CLIP fine-tuning improves transfer across held-out cell lines and chromosomes, but cross-assay transfer remains harder.

Biology+AI Daily · Research post

EnzymeRSI separates workflow gains from algorithm gains

EnzymeRSI evaluates research-workflow changes separately from changes to predictors and search algorithms, using matched budgets, sealed evaluations and recorded adoption decisions. Reported wet-lab studies cover luciferase activity, cytidine-deaminase substrate preference and polymerase incorporation of a modified nucleotide.

In the luciferase experiment, 10 of 32 non-prespecified candidates achieved at least twice wild-type activity. Within-pool ranking remained weak at Spearman 0.175, illustrating that selecting useful candidates and ranking them accurately are different achievements.

Biology+AI Daily · Research post

TALK plans experiments for a different prediction space

TALK selects measurable variants according to their expected reduction of uncertainty over a target set—for example, choosing single-mutant assays to improve predictions of higher-order mutants. Covariance-based greedy selection reduces redundant measurements without requiring labels during planning.

Retrospective ProteinGym-MM and GB1 evaluations report better target-space prediction than random, uncertainty-only and high-score selection. Conditional one-shot batches perform similarly to multiround feedback at equal budgets.

Biology+AI Daily · Research post

ZEUS scales symmetric protein-complex generation

ZEUS generates one canonical subunit and uses symmetry-transformed copies as context, reducing memory and runtime without retraining. It demonstrates symmetric assemblies exceeding 10,000 residues on one GPU, with similar computational design-quality distributions.

For models that break chain-permutation equivariance, the method’s equivalence is to an equivariant ablation rather than default inference.

Protein embeddings improve binary sequence-design representations

pLM-bVAE incorporates frozen protein-language-model embeddings into a binary variational autoencoder for quantum-annealing optimization. Tests on MHC-I peptides, trastuzumab antibody variants and AAV2 capsid variants report better sequence reconstruction and preservation of sequence relationships in binary space.

The searches yield more unique, novel sequences exceeding model-based score thresholds. ESM2 650M is the best overall encoder in the reported tests, rather than the larger 3B model.

CellSavior filters noise from single-cell DNA data

CellSavior is an open-source Python framework for extracting high-confidence cell barcodes from single-cell DNA-sequencing BAM files. It clusters across multiple distance metrics, then uses confidence-weighted merging to filter technical noise and produce consensus cell calls.

Shared by razoralign · Tool announcement

TeraMEM accelerates exact matching across genome assemblies

TeraMEM finds pairwise maximal exact matches using compressed indexing based on Burrows–Wheeler transform runs. A comparison with Mumemto reports up to 20-fold faster execution and 7.5-fold lower space use when index construction is included for both tools. Excluding construction, the reported speedup exceeds 2,500-fold.

Shared by razoralign · Research post

Faster k-mer lookup with spectral Burrows–Wheeler indexes

The SBWT work combines batching, prefetching, cache-efficient rank queries and skipped redundant searches to accelerate k-mer lookup roughly sevenfold over unbatched search.

For streaming 31-mer queries, a version using 12 bits per canonical k-mer reportedly outperforms SSHash on every tested read set, by up to 4.9-fold. Smaller variants trade speed for storage.

Français — Mouvement lycéen : une inquiétude grandissante pour la préparation des examens, les notes du premier trimestre et les dossiers Parcoursup

Le Monde · 12 novembre 2026 · Lire l’article

Après plusieurs semaines de mobilisation, certains lycéens s’interrogent sur le coût scolaire des blocages. Des élèves du lycée Jean-Jaurès, à Montreuil, évoquent leur inquiétude pour le baccalauréat. Même parmi ceux qui participent au mouvement, l’accumulation des cours manqués fait monter la pression : les programmes de spécialité laissent peu de marge pour rattraper un retard prolongé.

Le problème ne concerne pas seulement les épreuves finales. Les résultats du premier trimestre comptent aussi pour les candidatures sur Parcoursup, alors que certains élèves n’ont encore aucune note dans plusieurs disciplines. L’article met ainsi en lumière une tension entre engagement collectif et avenir individuel, à l’approche de la clôture du trimestre début décembre.

Pourquoi lire : Pour découvrir les conséquences concrètes d’une mobilisation sur la scolarité et mieux comprendre le vocabulaire de l’orientation dans le système éducatif français.

Vocabulaire utile

  • contrôle continu — évaluation fondée sur les résultats obtenus pendant l’année, plutôt que sur une seule épreuve finale.
  • Parcoursup — plateforme nationale de candidature à l’entrée dans l’enseignement supérieur français.
  • enseignement de spécialité — discipline choisie au lycée général et étudiée de manière approfondie en première et en terminale.

日本語 — フレームレート至上主義者が「GTA6」に備えて目をデチューンしてみた話

4Gamer · 2026-11-12 · 記事を読む

ゲームの映像は、見た目の豪華さと動きの滑らかさのどちらを優先したいだろうか。普段は120fps以上で遊び、必要なら影や反射などの設定を下げるという筆者が、「GTA6」に備えて30fpsの映像に慣れようと試みる体験記事。高フレームレートを重視してきた人が、あえて表示の滑らかさを抑えて遊ぶという逆向きの実験だ。

試す作品として登場するのは「The Blood of Dawnwalker」「Cyberpunk 2077」「GTA5」。記事は、手持ちのゲームを30fpsに制限して遊ぶ過程をたどり、自分の目がどのように慣れるのか、その経験から何に気づいたのかを扱う。性能の数値だけではなく、プレイヤーの感覚や設定へのこだわりを軸に読めるのが特徴だ。

読む理由： 身近なゲーム体験を通じて、映像技術の用語と感覚を表す日本語の両方に触れられる。

語彙メモ

  • フレームレート（ふれーむれーと） — 映像で1秒間に表示する画像の枚数。単位はfpsで、動きの滑らかさに関わる。
  • レイトレーシング（れいとれーしんぐ） — 光の進み方を計算し、反射や影などを表現する描画技術。
  • デチューン（でちゅーん） — 性能や設定をあえて下げること。この記事では、低いフレームレートに目を慣らす試みを比喩的に表している。

中文 — 中国“双十一”：民众消费仍谨慎 AI促销初显身手

DW 中文 · 2026-11-11 · 阅读原文

促销时间越拉越长，消费者却未必更愿意掏钱。德国之声这篇报道从今年“双十一”的阶段性销售表现切入，讨论房地产低迷、收入预期和消费信心之间的关系。受访研究人士指出，全年不断的打折活动也让消费者产生“折扣疲劳”，购物节的吸引力因而减弱；报道发布时的数据并不等于整个购物节的最终成绩。

不过，消费谨慎不意味着所有商品都卖不动。报道指出，与健康、睡眠及生活体验相关的产品仍有增长空间，消费者更看重实际需求和内在满足，而非昂贵商品带来的身份象征。这种变化让“消费降级”的故事多了一层含义：人们不仅在控制预算，也在重新排列消费的优先次序。

文章还介绍了电商平台引入人工智能的新尝试：淘宝、天猫接入“通义千问”，辅助处理订单并推荐商品，按词元计量的人工智能服务也进入直播销售场景。面对精打细算的顾客，平台正在尝试用新的技术和商品类别寻找增长机会。

为什么读： 这篇报道把日常购物、消费心理和人工智能商业化联系起来，适合练习中文经济与科技报道的常用表达。

词汇笔记

  • 消费降级 (xiāofèi jiàngjí) — 消费者减少非必需支出，或转向价格更低的商品与服务。
  • 同比增长 (tóngbǐ zēngzhǎng) — 与上一年相同时间段相比出现的增长，常用于观察销售和经济指标的变化。
  • 词元 (cíyuán) — 人工智能语言模型处理文本的基本单位，可对应一个字、词或词的一部分，也常用于计算服务用量。
