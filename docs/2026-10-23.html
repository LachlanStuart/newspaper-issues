Genomes, variation & evaluation

Carbon-X’s DNA–text release puts benchmark quality in focus

Carbon-X is described as a Qwen3-14B model midtrained on 300 billion tokens of DNA, English and interleaved data, with separate English BPE and DNA 6-mer tokenization and an Apache 2.0 release. Its developers report improvements on five DNA understanding and generation benchmarks.

The accompanying discussion raises the more consequential question: what do those benchmarks measure? Anshul Kundaje questions relevance, train–test leakage and shortcut learning, recommending evaluations from AlphaMissense, PromoterAI, AlphaGenome, SpliceAI/2 and TraitGym. Xinming Tu also calls for specialist-model comparisons. Claims of frontier-model parity and cost advantages remain developer claims here.

Source: Carbon-X announcement and discussion on X

hEVO trains on human haplotype diversity

The hEVO preprint describes 87M- and 319M-parameter genomic models trained on 4.784 billion distinct 1,024-base-pair haplotype sequences. Its corpus selects 63,809 individuals from eight cohorts to reduce European-ancestry dominance, followed by label-free allelic refinement of the prediction head.

The supplied digest reports strong functional-annotation and variant-prioritization benchmarks, including retrospective ClinVar reclassification. These results are not prospective clinical validation.

Source: hEVO research digest on X

T2G preserves RNA information during transcriptome-to-genome liftover

T2G transfers short- and long-read RNA-seq BAM alignments to genome coordinates while retaining alignment information and per-base RNA-modification calls. The supplied posts report better specificity and splice-junction accuracy than direct genome alignment, including fewer processed-pseudogene misassignments and preservation of isoform-specific modification signals. Numerical benchmarks are not supplied.

Source: T2G research post on X

Single-cell dynamics & integration

DynaVelo connects expression, regulatory activity and perturbations

DynaVelo is presented as a generative neural ODE model of single-cell gene expression and transcription-factor motif activity. It learns latent cell states, velocities and times, then derives state-dependent regulatory networks using Jacobians and in-silico perturbations. The paper title reports prediction of loss-of-function and rescue perturbations in B cells; the supplied posts contain no quantitative validation.

Source: DynaVelo research post on X

LoCoFlow follows neighborhoods, not just individual cells

LoCoFlow extends flow-based single-cell modeling from isolated expression vectors to local neighborhood point clouds. Its description combines probabilistic matching across time points with separate modeling of cell movement and neighborhood expansion, contraction and heterogeneity—an approach intended to support population-aware dynamics and fate inference. Benchmark outcomes are not included.

Source: LoCoFlow research post on X

CellDiffusion aligns batches through attention-guided graph diffusion

CellDiffusion builds a cell–cell graph using optimized mutual nearest neighbors across batches, then propagates transcription-derived features through attention-guided diffusion layers. The mechanism aims to weaken poor associations while aligning biologically related cells. Its claimed tolerance of incorrect graph links assumes those links are sparse relative to genuine matches; comparative results are unavailable in the supplied material.

Source: CellDiffusion research post on X

An updated Satija lab preprint with Caleb Lareau introduces RNA-marker enrichment before sequencing. The announcement mentions two updates, but the available excerpt reveals only this one; neither enrichment performance nor the second change is available.

Source: VIPerturb-seq update on X

Molecular discovery: what the scores establish

Nanobody benchmark separates binding prediction from epitope placement

A frozen ESM2 model with a small paired prediction module achieves reported sealed-test PR-AUC values of 0.999 for binding and 0.659 for paratopes. Epitope-residue prediction on novel antigens is much weaker: 0.070 against a 0.043 baseline.

On HER2, a separate structure-derived residue prior improves ranking once the correct domain is known. Sequence-only, structural-prior and competition-informed results need to remain distinct: excellent binding classification does not establish accurate epitope localization.

Source: nanobody benchmark digest on X

MADDIE coordinates molecular design and synthesis planning

MADDIE uses an orchestrator and specialized agents for hit identification, optimization, synthesis planning and evaluation. Across 50 targets, the digest reports 100 generated candidates per target satisfying the study’s computational property and synthesis-feasibility criteria. Ablations attribute gains to dynamic orchestration and explicit synthesis planning.

Docking scores, predicted toxicity and proposed routes remain proxies. The provided material does not establish experimental binding, safety or successful synthesis.

Source: MADDIE research digest on X

An immune world model distinguishes vaccine priming from tumor recognition

A peptide-vaccine framework explicitly separates vaccine-induced priming from recognition of the native tumor target. This prevents better presentation scores from standing in for missing functional-recognition evidence.

The system combines a mechanistic simulator, an evidence ledger and ranking tests on public dendritic-cell immunopeptidomics. Donor and antigen-source holdouts show different transfer behavior; synthetic controls test implemented dependencies, not therapeutic efficacy. Its five sequential research roles are executed by one principal agent.

Source: immune world-model digest on X

Helix Design Studio makes peptide-design trade-offs inspectable

This open-source browser tool scores and designs peptides on an assumed alpha-helical scaffold across idealized solvent and membrane-interface environments. It offers interpretable score components, competing-environment objectives and reproducible exports. Implementation checks support computational consistency—not antimicrobial potency, real-membrane behavior or host selectivity.

Source: Helix Design Studio digest on X

DEEP extracts mutation-sensitive potentials from structure-model distributions

DEEP converts frozen structure-predictor distance distributions into an explicit energy-like statistical potential without fitting experimental stability labels. The digest reports a mean per-assay Spearman correlation of 0.720 across 64 ProteinGym stability assays for DEEP-Boltz.

A comparison with AlphaFold 2 suggests that ranking native-like conformations and capturing mutation sensitivity are separable learned capabilities. These statistical potentials should not be read as experimentally established physical free energies.

Source: DEEP research digest on X

Carmack flags a robot-control and camera-timing mismatch

John Carmack highlights the mismatch between 50 Hz robot joint targets and 60 Hz video collection. He proposes matching robot control rates to video and adding a gen-lock-like trigger to synchronize their phases, while noting that this is not yet a leading problem for most robotics efforts.

Deutsch — »Habe mich keine Sekunde gelangweilt« – MW4 ist auf Steam das beste CoD seit 16 Jahren, dabei ist der Großteil noch nicht mal spielbar

GameStar · 20.10.2026 · Artikel lesen

Die Story-Kampagne von Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4 stößt laut GameStar bei den ersten Steam-Spielern auf ungewöhnlich viel Zustimmung. Zum im Artikel genannten Stand vom 20. Oktober waren 89 Prozent von über 3.900 Nutzerbewertungen positiv. Damit erreichte der neue Teil nach Darstellung des Berichts die beste Steam-Bewertung der Reihe seit dem ersten Black Ops aus dem Jahr 2010. Besonders gelobt werden das Erzähltempo und die abwechslungsreiche Inszenierung.

Entscheidend ist allerdings der Zeitpunkt dieser Momentaufnahme: Vorbesteller konnten die Kampagne bereits seit dem 16. Oktober spielen, während der Großteil des Mehrspielerangebots zum Berichtszeitpunkt noch nicht verfügbar war. Die frühen Bewertungen sind deshalb vor allem ein Stimmungsbild zum Einzelspielermodus und noch kein belastbares Urteil über das Gesamtpaket. Der Vergleich mit den deutlich schwächer bewerteten Vorgängern macht dennoch verständlich, weshalb dieser Auftakt innerhalb der langjährigen Shooter-Reihe auffällt.

Warum lesen: Der Artikel verbindet einen Einblick in die aktuelle Spielekultur mit der Frage, wie aussagekräftig frühe Nutzerbewertungen vor der vollständigen Veröffentlichung eines Spiels sind.

Français — Un tableau de Monet vendu 50 millions d’euros à Paris, record pour des enchères d’art en France

Le Monde · 22 octobre 2026 · https://www.lemonde.fr/culture/article/2026/10/22/un-tableau-de-monet-vendu-50-millions-d-euros-a-paris-record-pour-des-encheres-d-art-en-france_6798663_3246.html

Une toile de Claude Monet longtemps restée à l’abri des regards vient de battre un record sur le marché français. Vendue chez Sotheby’s à Paris, Iris a atteint 50 millions d’euros, frais inclus : selon la maison de vente, aucune œuvre d’art n’avait obtenu un tel prix aux enchères en France. Conservée dans la famille Schlumberger et connue seulement par une photographie en noir et blanc, cette peinture tardive était présentée au public pour la première fois.

L’article évoque aussi un autre destin possible pour les collections privées : trois Cézanne classés « trésors nationaux » ont été retirés des enchères, alors que la ville d’Aix-en-Provence envisage leur acquisition pour le Musée Granet avec le soutien du ministère de la culture. Ce bref compte rendu rapproche ainsi les prix spectaculaires du marché et les démarches visant à faire entrer des œuvres dans un musée.

Pourquoi lire : Une lecture courte pour découvrir une œuvre rarement vue et se familiariser avec le vocabulaire du marché de l’art et du patrimoine.

Vocabulaire utile

  • adjudication — attribution d’un bien à l’acheteur retenu à l’issue d’une vente aux enchères.
  • frais inclus — indication que le montant annoncé comprend les frais ajoutés au prix de vente.
  • trésor national — qualification juridique protégeant un bien culturel d’un intérêt majeur pour le patrimoine français, notamment en limitant sa sortie du territoire.

中文 — 用动作肉鸽，写一卷上古时代的洪荒史诗｜《定海》试玩体验

机核 · 2026-10-23 · https://www.gcores.com/articles/221358

《定海》如何把《山海经》变成一款反复闯关的动作游戏？这篇试玩体验从一对特殊搭档讲起：玄龙占据了益的身体，益则化为鸟形，与他一起寻找失踪的大禹。作者结合治水传说及古籍，讨论游戏如何改写人物关系，并把异兽、山川与神话碎片转化为关卡和收集系统，而不是仅仅借用几个熟悉的名字。

玩法部分围绕双武器衔接与法宝搭配展开。玩家可以在连招中发动“易武攻击”，改变距离和进攻节奏，再利用每轮获得的法宝强化不同打法。据点、天赋树与灵兽系统则提供跨局积累，让失败后的尝试也能留下进度。文章同时指出，新手阶段部分关键天赋开放较晚，可能让操作显得迟钝；大型敌人的身体也会遮挡视角，需要结合声音和地面提示判断攻击。

作者对试玩版的动作手感和神话改编持肯定态度，但仍期待正式版进一步丰富长期重复游玩的体验。文章最值得思考的问题是：一只神话异兽，能否同时拥有鲜明的战斗机制、影响玩法的能力，以及值得阅读的身世故事？

为什么读： 这篇文章把中国神话解读与具体的游戏设计分析结合起来，适合练习阅读文化评论和动作游戏评测。

词汇笔记

  • 肉鸽 (ròu gē) — 玩家对 Roguelike 类游戏的俗称，通常强调反复挑战、随机要素与每轮不同的玩法组合。
  • 构筑 (gòu zhù) — 通过搭配武器、技能、装备及强化效果，形成有协同作用的战斗方案。
  • 无敌帧 (wú dí zhēn) — 翻滚或闪避等动作中，角色短暂免受伤害的判定时段。
