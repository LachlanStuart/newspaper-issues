Nullsettes benchmark tests whether genomic language models understand expression logic

Nullsettes creates synthetic loss-of-function expression cassettes by rearranging regulatory and coding elements while preserving the original parts. Across 14 genomic language models, many failed to reliably score obviously broken constructs below intact ones, especially when the intact cassette was evolutionarily implausible. The result is a warning that sequence likelihood may track naturalness more than mechanistic gene-expression function.
