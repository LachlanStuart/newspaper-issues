Lead Stories

HoloCell: a generative foundation model for single-cell multi-omics

HoloCell is described as an 860M-parameter model for holistic cellular modeling across single-cell epigenomics, transcriptomics, and proteomics. The reported design uses gene-anchored hierarchical tokens for cis-regulatory elements, genes, and proteins, combines bidirectional attention with diffusion-based generation, and was pretrained on roughly 468 million single-cell profiles.

Source: X Bio Research

OpenCLIP adds NaFlex, generative captioning variants, and modern text towers

Ross Wightman merged a broad OpenCLIP update covering NaFlex CLIP image towers for variable-resolution and variable-aspect token-budget batching, NaFlex CLAP for variable-duration audio-text training, generative image and audio captioning variants, modern RoPE/SwiGLU/RMSNorm text towers, variable-length text, and Hugging Face ModernBERT tower support.

AI for Biology and Genomics

New methods, models, and benchmarks for molecular biology, single-cell analysis, and genomics.

Promera unifies co-folding, filtering, and controllable biomolecular design

Promera is presented as an all-atom model for structure prediction, binder filtering, and controllable biomolecular design. The thread claims strong performance on protein-protein, antibody-antigen, and protein-ligand categories, with epitope/paratope conditioning for targeted binder design across proteins, RNA, DNA, and small molecules.

drug discovery protein design binder design

BioNeuralNet uses GNNs for multi-omics disease modeling

BioNeuralNet is described as a graph-neural-network tool for converting multi-omics networks into low-dimensional biological embeddings. Its DPMON disease-prediction workflow combines adjacency networks, multi-omics data, and optional clinical covariates across GCN, GAT, GraphSAGE, and GIN architectures.

Anshul Kundaje argues that the real question is not whether scaling laws exist in cell biology, but whether scaled models deliver downstream value that justifies their cost over simpler alternatives. The comment lands amid ongoing debate around virtual-cell and single-cell foundation models.

Single-Cell Methods and Biological Interpretation

ScEnsemble: hypergraph ensemble clustering for scRNA-seq

ScEnsemble represents cluster co-assignments as weighted hyperedges and partitions them with consensus algorithms such as weighted CSPA, MCLA, and hypergraph spectral clustering.

scCRAFT+ integrates scRNA-seq with virtual adversarial learning

scCRAFT+ is a semi-supervised integration model that uses marker-gene information and Virtual Adversarial Training to improve robustness to noisy or incomplete annotations.

DRVI seeks interpretable latent factors for cellular heterogeneity

DRVI is described as a single-cell interpretability method that disentangles cellular heterogeneity into biological factors through structured latent representations.

Sparse autoencoders probe AF3 and Boltz-2 denoising trajectories

A paper highlighted in the run trains sparse autoencoders on AlphaFold 3 versus Boltz-2 denoising trajectories, pointing toward interpretability for biomolecular structure-generation models.
