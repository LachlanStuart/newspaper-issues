Molecular design: experiments and predictions

Enzyme design · X Bio Research

AlphaProtein Novo tackles unusual chemistry—with important hit-rate caveats

A supplied preprint summary describes catalytic-motif-conditioned enzyme design across five reactions, including selective piperidine synthesis and partial DEHP hydrolysis under harsh conditions. Mechanism-aware filtering and checking whether multiple redesigned sequences preserve catalytic geometry reportedly improve prospective experimental hit rates.

The distinction to keep: the higher 60–80% figures describe retrospective enrichment, not campaign-wide prospective success. Researchers also raise an unresolved practical question: when is extensive computational filtering cheaper than simply testing more candidates?

Access remains unclear. One account describes non-commercial access to code and weights; a later shared post says the code is on GitHub but the weights are not open.

MIMOSA guides antibody generation using known interaction geometry

MIMOSA steers RFAntibody and BoltzGen at inference time toward selected hotspots from known binder–target structures, without retraining. The supplied summary reports experimental binder hit rates of roughly 20–26% across KEAP1, uPA and IL-8 campaigns, including targets where unconstrained BoltzGen cohorts yielded no binders.

Best reported apparent affinities include 37 nM for KEAP1 and 4.1 nM for IL-8. The method depends on a structurally characterized interaction; preserving hotspot chemistry can help, but can also overconstrain a design.

MIMOSA — source post

GuideFlip targets flexible proteins and specific receptor states

GuideFlip combines an inverse-folding prior with repeated complex prediction so binder sequence and flexible interface geometry evolve together. Reported experiments include an RBX1 binder reaching 4.5 nM in a second round and 12 of 16 designed nanobodies binding active β1-adrenergic receptor without detectable inactive-state binding. A cryo-EM structure supports the designed GPCR interface.

For α-synuclein, seven binders mean 7/52 tested designs or 7/37 well-expressed designs—not interchangeable success rates. The released disordered-proteome candidates remain predictions, not a validated binding atlas.

GuideFlip — source post

OFoldNA brings pairing information into nucleic-acid folding

OFoldNA learns secondary-structure representations for DNA/RNA folding and protein–nucleic-acid cofolding, optionally accepting partial or complete pairing maps. The supplied account reports improvements on nucleic-acid monomers and protein–DNA interfaces, with further gains from accurate pairing information.

Examples include strand-specific DNA recognition and a bridge-RNA recombinase assembly. Results using supplied pairing maps should be read separately from sequence-only performance.

OFoldNA — source post

RNA-IFlow designs sequences for target secondary structures

Structure-conditioned flow matching generates RNA sequences; an RL variant refines them with thermodynamic folding feedback and structure-preserving updates. The summary reports Rfam-27 Pass@1 of 85.19% and Pass@8 of 87.65%, alongside improved target probability and ensemble defect.

These are computational folding benchmarks. Experimental RNA-function validation is not described.

RNA-IFlow — source post

RF-PepTune protects peptide geometry during property optimization

RF-PepTune screens substitutions and their pairwise compatibility before optimizing predicted permeability, solubility, non-fouling and low hemolysis. Across 334 parent peptides targeting MDM2, GABARAP and MCL1, reported joint property-and-structure success rises from 10.2% without gating to 77.5% with pair-complete gating.

Both the property improvements and structural checks are model-based, not experimental validation.

RF-PepTune — source post

AtomWeaver extends peptide design to non-canonical residues

AtomWeaver generates side-chain atom clouds before assigning identities from an expandable residue library, avoiding generator retraining. Its reported 300-residue library includes 280 non-canonical amino acids, with evaluations against PUMA–MCL1 and CP2–KDM4A mutational scans.

Refolding checks support computational structural consistency; they do not establish experimental binding by newly generated designs.

AtomWeaver — source post

CAGenMol-2 combines molecular prediction, generation and editing

One masked diffusion model handles property prediction, property- and pocket-conditioned generation, and constrained editing. Its AdaFO fragment-optimization loop raises reported CrossDocked2020 success from 30.2% to 70.8% under the authors’ computational protocol.

Confidence estimates are ranking signals, not calibrated probabilities. CRBN/VHL examples end in computational prioritization, not experimentally confirmed compounds.

CAGenMol-2 — source post

AbGaze uses orientation-aware antigen surfaces

Antigen-surface distance, direction and orientation guide antibody refinement and multi-CDR design. A reported cluster-decontaminated, 60-complex RAbD benchmark shows gains in sequence recovery and interface geometry over compared methods.

Affinity-optimization gains come from a frozen prediction model rather than binding assays.

AbGaze — source post

GraPPI learns representations of molecular interactions

Residue-level sequence embeddings, complex geometry and masked-edge pretraining support interface classification, absolute-affinity prediction and mutation-associated affinity changes. Reported correlations on independent S90/S79 affinity benchmarks are approximately 0.69.

The highlighted SKEMPI correlation of 0.84 is a best-fold result, not an aggregate benchmark score.

GraPPI — source post

Katalyst aligns enzymes, reactions and text for turnover prediction

Katalyst uses contrastive learning to align enzyme sequences, functional descriptions, substrates and products for kcat prediction. Frozen pretrained encoders are connected through trainable adapters.

The supplied posts give no benchmark results or experimental validation, leaving predictive performance unassessed here.

Katalyst — source post

Virtual-cell debate puts metrics and experimental design before scale

Anshul Kundaje welcomes a study of performance metrics and data issues but argues these checks should precede large-model claims. Baselines, he notes, could also benefit from weighted mean-squared-error training. Related posts warn that losses can reward uninformative predictions while suppressing perturbation-specific signal.

The larger challenge is mechanistic: current experimental designs and measured inputs do not, in his view, establish mechanistic virtual cells. The study and most of the thread are absent from the supplied material, so this is a methodological debate—not a settled comparison of model performance.

PIE predicts perturbation effects with experimental priors

PIE—Perturbation is Everything—combines biological knowledge, baseline expression and observed perturbation responses to predict differentially expressed genes and expression changes. Latent vectors integrate knowledge-source tokens before gene embeddings query the representation.

Its inputs include DepMap dependency profiles. Consequently, an “unseen perturbation” can be experimentally characterized already, with only its transcriptional response unmeasured—not an intervention lacking experimental information altogether.

PIE — source post

SHERLOCK structures perturbations for causal cellular modeling

This interpretable deep generative framework represents genetic and pharmacological perturbations as structured interventions on a latent baseline cellular state. Sparse, correlated representations organize interventions by shared transcriptional responses.

Its structural causal model supports counterfactual estimation under explicit identifiability assumptions. The excerpts neither supply quantitative benchmarks nor establish unrestricted causal recovery.

SHERLOCK — source post

VENUSREM-HARNESS examines protein-model bias

A training-free inference layer combines family-specific sequence evidence, gated correction of global amino-acid preferences and structural-reliability weighting. The supplied summary reports broad improvements across 1,211 assays and approximately 3.1 million measured variants.

The central diagnosis is a conflict between corpus-wide preferences and local evolutionary constraints. Benefits remain phenotype-dependent; some assay categories show little or negative improvement.

VENUSREM-HARNESS — source post

Multi-ISM addresses the cost of saturation mutagenesis

Multi-ISM targets the millions of model evaluations needed to examine mutations in long-context sequence-to-function models. A related post describes an architecture-agnostic approach to estimating and denoising saturation-mutagenesis effects in sequence-to-omics models.

Implementation details, accuracy measurements and speedup figures are not included.

Multi-ISM — source post

scXMatch tests distribution shifts in single-cell data

This Python package implements Rosenbaum’s cross-match test through distance-based matching of high-dimensional single-cell groups. The supplied description reports tests against experimental perturbations of varying strengths to distinguish biological signal from noise.

Dataset sizes and quantitative performance are unavailable.

Deutsch — Warum ein Studium keine Jobgarantie mehr ist

Tagesschau · 06.10.2026 · Artikel lesen

Ein Hochschulabschluss schützt nicht automatisch vor Arbeitslosigkeit: Laut dem Beitrag waren 2025 in Deutschland rund 335.000 Menschen mit Uniabschluss arbeitslos. Ihre Arbeitslosenquote lag mit 3,3 Prozent zwar weiterhin unter dem allgemeinen Durchschnitt, ist aber gegenüber der Zeit vor der Pandemie deutlich gestiegen. Der Arbeitsmarktforscher Enzo Weber verweist auf die wirtschaftliche Krise, die gewachsene Zahl der Studierenden und den Wandel klassischer Büroarbeit durch künstliche Intelligenz.

Gleichzeitig fehlen in vielen Ausbildungsberufen Fachkräfte, etwa in der Elektrotechnik, im Transportwesen und in der Pflege. Der Artikel stellt deshalb nicht nur monatliche Gehälter gegenüber, sondern berücksichtigt auch den früheren Berufseinstieg: Auszubildende verdienen bereits Geld und zahlen Rentenbeiträge, während andere noch studieren. Akademiker erzielen im Schnitt weiterhin höhere Lebenseinkommen, doch Fachrichtung und individueller Berufsweg machen einen erheblichen Unterschied.

Als weitere Möglichkeit beschreibt der Beitrag das duale Studium, das betriebliche Praxis, Hochschullehre und ein laufendes Gehalt verbindet. Eine pauschale Empfehlung gegen das Studium ergibt sich daraus nicht. Entscheidend seien vielmehr Fachwissen, praktische Erfahrung und die Bereitschaft, neue digitale Werkzeuge sinnvoll einzusetzen – unabhängig davon, über welchen Bildungsweg man in den Beruf kommt.

Warum lesen: Der Artikel bietet einen differenzierten Blick auf Berufswahl in Deutschland und zeigt, warum Gehalt, Jobsicherheit und KI nicht mit der einfachen Formel „Studium schlägt Ausbildung“ zu erfassen sind.

Français — Au-delà de la mobilisation lycéenne, une bataille du récit entre LFI et le gouvernement

France 24 · 6 octobre 2026 · Lire l’article

Les lycéens mobilisés réclament davantage de personnel et de moyens matériels, sur fond de bâtiments dégradés et de classes surchargées. Mais leurs revendications s’accompagnent désormais d’un affrontement politique sur la manière d’interpréter le mouvement. Cette analyse de Romain Brunet examine deux discours concurrents : le gouvernement accuse La France insoumise d’encourager les débordements, tandis que le parti met en avant les violences policières et cherche à élargir la mobilisation à d’autres luttes sociales.

À l’approche de l’élection présidentielle, chaque camp s’adresse aussi à son électorat. Le politologue Bruno Cautrès, interrogé dans l’article, considère que le soutien d’un parti à un mouvement social relève du fonctionnement politique normal. Le gouvernement entend faire avancer son projet de loi « casseurs-payeurs » ; Jean-Luc Mélenchon appelle, de son côté, élus et militants à protéger les jeunes manifestants. L’article rapporte également l’ouverture d’enquêtes de l’Inspection générale de la police nationale après des interventions ayant fait des blessés graves.

La journée du 6 octobre apparaît ainsi comme un test pour la mobilisation et pour l’opinion publique. L’appel de Mélenchon à porter des casques ou des brassards blancs renvoie aux manifestations étudiantes de 1986, marquées par la mort de Malik Oussekine. Cette référence historique éclaire l’enjeu central du texte : au-delà des événements eux-mêmes, les acteurs politiques cherchent à imposer leur lecture de la crise et de ses responsabilités.

Pourquoi lire : Une analyse qui permet de comprendre les stratégies de communication politique autour d’un mouvement social français tout en travaillant le vocabulaire des institutions et de la contestation.

Vocabulaire utile

  • récupération politique — utilisation d’une mobilisation ou d’un événement pour servir les intérêts d’un parti ou d’un responsable politique.
  • maintien de l’ordre — ensemble des dispositifs et interventions destinés à préserver la sécurité publique, notamment pendant les manifestations.
  • force d’interposition — groupe qui se place entre des parties opposées pour empêcher ou limiter les affrontements.

日本語 — ミスターマックスで不正アクセス 個人情報最大170万人超流出か

NHKニュース · 2026年10月6日 · 記事を読む

ディスカウントストアのミスターマックスで、買い物用アプリとオンラインストアを支えるサーバーが10月3日夕方に不正アクセスを受け、会員情報が外部に流出した。登録会員173万人余り全員が対象となるおそれがあり、ID、氏名、メールアドレス、電話番号などが流出した可能性があるという。

会社側は、パスワードや住所、生年月日、クレジットカード情報は流出しておらず、情報の不正利用による被害も現時点では確認していないと説明している。外部からのアクセスを遮断する措置を取り、アプリなどは利用できる状態だが、会員には、流出情報を使ったなりすましやフィッシングのメールへの警戒を求めている。

読む理由： 身近な買い物サービスの情報流出を通じて、被害の可能性と確認済みの事実を区別する報道表現や、情報セキュリティーの語彙を学べる。

語彙メモ

  • 不正アクセス（ふせいアクセス） — 正当な権限を持たずに、コンピューターやシステムへ入り込むこと。
  • 情報流出（じょうほうりゅうしゅつ） — 管理されている情報が、意図せず外部に漏れること。
  • フィッシング — 実在する企業などを装い、偽のサイトへ誘導して認証情報などをだまし取る手口。

中文 — 澳洲监管机构：热门游戏平台儿童保护存在明显缺口

联合早报 · 2026-10-05 · https://www.zaobao.com.sg/news/world/story20261005-9786891

游戏平台不仅是娱乐空间，也是儿童与陌生人接触的社交场所。据《联合早报》引述的报道，澳洲电子安全委员会发现，Steam、Roblox、Fortnite和Minecraft在防范儿童性剥削、暴力极端内容等网络伤害方面存在漏洞，而且各平台的保护措施差异明显。

报告关注的关键问题包括有害内容识别、年龄验证和跨平台合作。Steam母公司Valve被指未采用业界常用技术，主动识别和移除已知有害内容；Minecraft和Fortnite则在多数情况下依赖用户自行申报年龄，儿童可能通过声称成年而使用风险较高的通讯功能。报道也提到，Steam近期加入信用卡年龄验证，Roblox则已加强年龄检查。

这份报告依据2025年10月至2026年3月收集的资料完成。澳洲虽已禁止16岁以下儿童使用部分社交媒体，游戏平台却不在禁令范围内。文章由此呈现了一个值得关注的监管问题：当游戏与社交功能交织，儿童保护不能只着眼于传统社交媒体。

为什么读： 这篇报道把游戏文化与网络治理联系起来，适合学习有关平台责任、儿童保护和身份核验的新闻表达。

词汇笔记

  • 年龄验证 (niánlíng yànzhèng) — 通过证件、支付资料等方式核实用户年龄，而非仅接受用户自行填写的岁数。
  • 性剥削 (xìng bōxuē) — 利用他人的弱势处境或权力不对等，使其遭受与性有关的侵害或被用来牟利。
  • 激进化 (jījìnhuà) — 在本文语境中，指个人逐渐接受极端观念，甚至认同或支持暴力的过程。
