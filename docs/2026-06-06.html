Front Page

ESMFold2 scaling depends on perturbing pair representations, with API/default fixes pending

Roshan Rao notes that reproducing ESMFold2 scaling behavior depends on several stochastic settings that are not yet exposed cleanly in the public API: random LM dropout masks, random LM input masks, MSA subsampling, and MSA column masking. The team is working on open-source/API fixes and better defaults, with exact settings currently documented in the supplement.

This is the kind of detail that matters if ESMFold2 is being used downstream for binder-design behavior or benchmark comparisons: the “model” is not just the weights, but also the perturbation recipe around inference/training-like sampling.

Agentic Biology & Design Loops

AgentPLM: protein language models with reasoning-augmented decoding and tool calls

AgentPLM reframes protein sequence design as an agentic loop: the model can pause during decoding, call biophysical oracles such as ESMFold, FoldX, or AutoDock Vina, then continue generation with updated context. Its Reasoning-Augmented Decoding adds tool-call actions and uses structural self-consistency as an uncertainty trigger.

https://x.com/BiologyAIDaily/status/2061792214389580199

protein design agentic AI PLMs

PROBE: probing-guided molecular optimization for LLM agents in structure-based drug design

PROBE proposes a “probe before you edit” workflow for LLM-guided molecular optimization. It builds pocket-specific site maps, diagnoses editable molecular sites, runs analog perturbations, and constructs an EditManual to reduce objective interference between binding affinity and druggability.

https://x.com/BiologyAIDaily/status/2062185295999889863

drug design LLM agents molecular optimization

Site4Drug: AI-agent target-site selection for drug discovery

Site4Drug focuses on deciding where to intervene on a protein, not only what molecule binds. It ranks targetable regions with constraints, evidence summaries, risk flags, and a decision log, while also recommending modality from the same evidence.

The interesting bit is upstream auditability: accessibility, topology, PTMs, motifs, domains, and feasibility signals are surfaced before the project commits to a target site.

https://x.com/BiologyAIDaily/status/2061792037524095345

Tamarind Bio assay portal connects computational protein design to wet-lab validation

Tamarind Bio announced an assay portal with partners including A-Alpha Bio, Adaptyv Bio, and Ginkgo. The pitch is lab-in-the-loop validation for protein and antibody design: binding affinity, developability, expression, stability, model specialization, and assay-derived training data.

Protein Interfaces, Antibodies & Binding Sites

ProSiteHunter: sequence-only prediction of protein-DNA, protein-RNA, PPI, and antibody-antigen binding sites

ProSiteHunter proposes a unified sequence-based framework for residue-level binding-site prediction across protein-DNA, protein-RNA, protein-protein, and antibody-antigen interfaces. It combines task-adapted PLM features, structure-related embeddings, predicted geometry, physicochemical/statistical features, multi-source fusion, and interaction-learning blocks.

https://x.com/BiologyAIDaily/status/2062185628780085437

binding sites protein interfaces PLMs

DeepRank-Ab: graph-based scoring for antibody-antigen complex ranking

DeepRank-Ab targets a very practical antibody-modeling failure mode: good antibody-antigen poses may be sampled but ranked poorly. The work builds a curated decoy benchmark from SAbDab/HADDOCK3, trains continuous DockQ prediction, and finds that atom-level interface graphs plus Voronoi contact features and antibody-specific annotations improve ranking.

https://x.com/BiologyAIDaily/status/2062185544751448443

antibodies structure prediction ranking

AbLingua: scaling antibody language models for structure-aware antibody engineering

AbLingua pretrains antibody language models with overlapping tripeptide units to better capture local cooperative interactions and CDR conformations. The work scales an antibody-specific encoder up to 1.7B parameters on 1.4B antibody sequences from OAS, aiming at structure-aware representations for antibody engineering.

https://x.com/BiologyAIDaily/status/2059994414194491437

antibodies foundation models therapeutics

SwitchCraft: programmatic design of state-switching proteins

SwitchCraft designs proteins that switch between functional states in response to ligands or binding contexts. Users specify multiple states and differentiable constraints, then optimize one sequence through Boltz-1 across all state contexts, with losses for motifs, binding, anti-binding, conformational change, and related objectives.

This one is especially biological-circuit-adjacent: explicit multistate, ligand-responsive switching rather than static structure generation.

Drug Discovery Evaluation, Phenotypes & ADMET

HonestAffinity: leak-aware evaluation changes whether protein/pocket priors help affinity models

HonestAffinity argues that ESM-2 protein embeddings and pocket-position markers can help on familiar PDBbind/CASF-style splits but hurt under stricter leak-proof LP-PDBBind tiers. The broader point is deployment-conditioned model choice: priors that help on familiar annotated targets may mislead under strict generalization.

https://x.com/BiologyAIDaily/status/2062185419308249418

affinity prediction data leakage evaluation

PhAME: phenotype-aware molecular editing with latent diffusion

PhAME frames hit-to-lead as latent-space molecular editing that steers compounds toward high-dimensional phenotypic targets, such as transcriptomic or morphology profiles, while staying close to a seed molecule. It uses compositional classifier-free guidance to combine phenotype and similarity constraints.

https://x.com/BiologyAIDaily/status/2060386371827884091

phenotypic screening diffusion models hit-to-lead

Mapping the avoid-ome: open-science predictive ADMET targets

The “avoid-ome” frames ADMET attrition as a recurring set of protein liabilities behind absorption, distribution, metabolism, excretion, and toxicity failures. The useful takeaway is that better predictive ADMET needs consistent mechanistic data at least as much as it needs better ML models.

Genomics, RNA & Regulatory Models

Carbon: long-context generative DNA language models up to roughly 786 kbp

CARBON introduces decoder-only generative DNA language models at 3B and 8B scale using non-overlapping 6-mer tokens, long-context training, and YaRN extrapolation. The 8B model reaches 131,072 tokens, corresponding to roughly 786 kbp of DNA context.

The emphasis on data curation, tokenization, objectives, and context length makes it relevant for long-range regulatory sequence modeling, not just generic “bigger genomics model” news.

Orthus: RNA foundation model trained with splicing isoforms and orthologous transcripts

Nature Methods highlights Orthus, an RNA foundation model pretrained via contrastive learning over splicing isoforms and orthologous transcripts. The model targets RNA representation learning using evolutionary and isoform relationships rather than plain sequence modeling alone.

CREsted is a Nature Methods toolbox for analysis, modeling, and design of cell-type-specific enhancers. It is directly relevant to regulatory-sequence modeling and enhancer design workflows.

Systems Biology & Proteome-Scale Modeling

ProteomeLM models whole proteomes for PPI and gene essentiality prediction

ProteomeLM is a transformer over entire proteomes rather than single proteins or local windows. It uses pretrained protein embeddings to learn organism-level constraints and predict protein-protein interactions and gene essentiality across taxa, including microbial essentiality settings.
