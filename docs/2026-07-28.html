Top Stories

Deep learning finds conserved ageing states across mice and humans

A residual domain-adversarial network transfers ageing-state labels from mouse to human single-cell data while suppressing species-specific shortcuts. Reported held-out human AUROCs range from 0.933 to 0.953 across stem-cell transcriptomic, epigenomic and CD8+ T-cell datasets. Multiple attribution methods identify conserved genes and pathways, while ablations connect the architecture and training scheme to transfer accuracy and attribution stability.

Single-cell biology · Ageing · Domain adaptation · Interpretability

Spatium trains on 51.8 million spatial-proteomics cells

The foundation model draws on 70 human studies spanning four platforms, 18 tissues and 18 disease contexts. Rank-based protein tokenization is intended to handle platform-specific intensity scales and incomplete panels. Reported uses include few-shot cell annotation, spatial-niche discovery, protein imputation and transfer among spatial proteomics, single-cell RNA sequencing and histology.

Spatial proteomics · Foundation models · Multimodal biology

DECODE learns biological-response embeddings for virtual screening

DECODE trains jointly on chemical structures, L1000 transcriptomics and Cell Painting profiles, yet can screen from chemical structure alone at inference. It separates shared response factors from modality-specific signals and uses contrastive alignment plus modality masking. Evaluations reportedly include mechanism-of-action retrieval and early enrichment in anticancer and target-specific screens.

Drug discovery · Virtual screening · Multimodal learning

Explicit ions can steer AlphaFold 3—but not reliably

A perspective explores adding explicit ions to sample alternative conformations and diagnose over-folded disordered regions. Examples show major structural changes in RNA–protein interfaces, G-quadruplexes, membrane proteins and disordered sequences, but also incorrect stoichiometries, clashes and experimentally inconsistent states. The central warning: confidence scores reflect internal certainty, not correctness.

AlphaFold 3 · Structural biology · RNA · Model evaluation
