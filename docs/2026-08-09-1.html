Biology AI Research

DoxSand: a designed protein fold that sequesters doxorubicin

A motif-guided generative workflow produced an 85-residue protein that binds the anticancer drug doxorubicin through an aromatic tryptophan sandwich. Iterative loop and packing changes improved affinity from the micromolar range to roughly 85 nM while improving monomeric behavior.

A 1.56 Å crystal structure closely matched the designed complex. In ovarian-cancer cells, the binder reduced doxorubicin cytotoxicity by shifting its apparent IC50 about 140-fold. Structural searches found no significant homologs, suggesting a previously unobserved compact fold.

Biology AI Research

Input screening as a biosecurity layer for protein-design systems

This proposal screens a requested binding target before protein generation, rather than trying to identify hazardous novel binders afterward. It compares input sequences with a database of 14,541 potentially harmful human proteoforms using ESM-C embeddings.

The baseline screen reached 97.2% F1 and remained sensitive to function-preserving mutations, although broad dual-use classification would also flag many legitimate therapeutic targets. Red-teaming found that long disordered tails could evade mean-pooled screening; overlapping-window screening recovered all tested evasions.

Biology AI Research

Distribution constraints curb reward hacking in 5′ UTR design

Unconstrained genetic optimization frequently pushed 5′ UTR candidates outside a translation predictor’s validated distribution: 72–96% of final-generation designs exceeded an embedding-distance threshold.

A hard nearest-neighbor trust region kept designs in distribution while preserving predicted mean ribosome load and produced about 4.3 times more selectable candidates than post-hoc filtering. The authors emphasize that “reliable” means in-distribution here, not experimentally validated.

Biology AI Research

HANSEN combines structural and resistance data for leprosy drug discovery

HANSEN is a web-accessible atlas of all 1,603 proteins in Mycobacterium leprae. It combines multiple structure predictors, 726 modeled oligomeric assemblies, confidence maps, pocket annotations, ligand context, transferred essentiality predictions, and a multi-parameter target-priority score.

Its Lep-AMR component analyzes nanopore amplicons for resistance mutations in rpoB, folP1, and gyrA, linking eligible variants to structural interpretation.

Single-Cell Research

scDMC uses dual expression encodings

scDMC is described as a dual-stream single-cell foundation model. It processes a rank-based gene sequence and an absolute-expression-aware sequence in parallel, combining gene-level masked language modeling with cell-level momentum contrastive learning.

The design aims to preserve both local gene context and global cellular representations that can be lost when a model relies on a single encoding scheme.

Single-Cell Research

scATrans separates transcription from RNA stabilization

scATrans augments conventional single-cell differential-expression analysis with reference-corrected unspliced-RNA residuals. Its goal is to distinguish expression changes weighted toward transcription from those weighted toward RNA stabilization.

The method occupies a middle ground between static differential expression and RNA velocity, favoring comparisons between conditions without strong claims about trajectories or kinetic identifiability.

Biology AI Research

ProtFinder speeds protein phylogenetic model selection

ProtFinder predicts substitution matrices, empirical-frequency settings, and among-site rate-heterogeneity models for protein alignments. Training progresses from simulated alignments to mixed simulated and real data, followed by real-only fine-tuning.

Reported inference is up to 1,400 times faster than ModelFinder. On an independent real-alignment set, its substitution-model component reached 67% top-one and 90.96% top-two agreement, supporting its use as a rapid screening stage before maximum-likelihood confirmation.

Français — Après le crash d’une fusée SpaceX sur la Lune, les spécialistes tiraillés entre l’opportunité scientifique et l’absence de cadre juridique

Le Monde · 8 août 2026 · Article original

L’étage supérieur d’une fusée Falcon 9 de SpaceX s’est écrasé sur la Lune le 5 août, près du cratère Einstein. Resté dans l’espace après une mission lancée en janvier 2025, ce débris de quatre tonnes aurait été progressivement placé sur une trajectoire lunaire sous l’effet combiné de l’activité solaire et des forces gravitationnelles. Des observations effectuées depuis le Chili et par une sonde sud-coréenne ont confirmé la collision.

L’impact, bien qu’involontaire, représente une occasion d’étude pour les scientifiques : les caractéristiques de l’objet étant connues, ils pourront analyser la formation du cratère, les matériaux projetés et les méthodes de suivi des objets spatiaux. L’événement suscite toutefois un débat, car cette opportunité scientifique résulte d’un accident et met en évidence l’insuffisance du cadre juridique applicable aux débris et aux impacts sur la Lune.

Pourquoi lire : L’article associe astronomie, gestion des débris spatiaux et droit international dans un cas concret particulièrement actuel.

Vocabulaire utile

  • étage supérieur — partie haute d’une fusée qui poursuit le vol après la séparation du propulseur principal
  • débris spatial — objet artificiel devenu inutile et demeurant dans l’espace
  • alunisseur — engin spatial conçu pour se poser sur la surface de la Lune

中文 — 休达移民潮酿西义外交争端　欧盟：双边边境管制可望很快解除

RFI 中文 · 2026-08-09 · 原文

西班牙开始对从意大利入境的旅客实施边境管制，回应意大利早前针对西班牙旅客采取的类似措施。争端源于西班牙北非飞地休达出现大规模移民越境；意大利以持续的非法移民压力为由，暂时恢复内部边境检查，西班牙随后采取对等措施。
