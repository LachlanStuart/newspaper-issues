AI-redesigned proteins give directed evolution a stronger starting point

A Nature study pairs ProteinMPNN redesign with continuous directed evolution. Added stability allowed botulinum-neurotoxin proteases to tolerate mutations that improved activity, expression and specificity; one ataxin-2-targeting variant achieved more than 79-fold greater selected specificity than the best variant evolved from wild type. Whether the result generalizes beyond this protein family remains open.

Can general-purpose language models design physically plausible 3D ligands?

The 3D-Fit benchmark asks LLMs to generate reconstructable molecules from textual descriptions of pockets, fragments and mandatory interactions. Models often obeyed local constraints, but produced strained or clashing poses and remained behind specialized diffusion systems on physical plausibility. The authors also flag dual-use risks as instruction following improves.

Long RNA structure prediction from sequence alone

NUMonomer is a quadratic-scaling, all-atom model for RNA and single-stranded DNA that needs no MSA, secondary-structure annotation or pretrained sequence embedding. Tests included RNA structures as long as 4,269 nucleotides; it was competitive with AlphaFold 3 and stronger in strict sequence-only comparisons, while ssDNA remained difficult for every method.

ABOPD learns from antibody diffusion’s own trajectories

ABOPD tackles exposure bias by distilling a backbone-aware teacher on intermediate states encountered during the student model’s reverse-denoising rollouts. External CDR-H3 and six-CDR evaluations found lower backbone RMSD and fewer steric clashes than controls trained only on reference-derived noisy states.

ProtSyntax models the grammar of protein modification

ProtSyntax is a sparse mixture-of-experts model trained on 4.25 million examples spanning 40 post-translational-modification classes. Its objectives also cover kinase specificity, modification crosstalk and enzyme kinetics, with reported applications to variant prioritization and Alzheimer’s-pathway proteins.

Drug Discovery & Molecular Modeling

DBMol optimizes molecules through structure predictors

DBMol alternates gradient optimization in a relaxed molecular-graph space with projection back to valid chemistry. It uses Boltz-2 objectives and evaluates with held-out AlphaFold 3, docking, diversity and retrosynthesis analyses. Predictor-aligned success improved, but chemical repair and synthesis mapping sometimes damaged pocket fit.

Calibrating uncertainty across binding-affinity engines

RELIABLE-BA fuses GNINA, BIND, FlowDock and DynamicBind as evidential experts, estimating reliability from protein and ligand embeddings. On PDBbind and an independent BindingDB-derived set, choosing the most confident 10% of predictions reduced mean absolute error by as much as 25.7%.
