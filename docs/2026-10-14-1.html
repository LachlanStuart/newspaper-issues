Drug discovery & proteins

Prompteina: text-to-protein generation with experimental binders

Prompteina conditions all-atom protein generation on natural-language descriptions, combining global text embeddings with residue-specific cross-attention. Its reported training dataset pairs approximately 1.8 million structures with 52.4 million LLM-written captions.

The supplied summary reports nine GM2A binders among 89 expressed and surface-plasmon-resonance-assayed designs, with a strongest apparent KD of 107 nM. That is binding evidence beyond computational metrics—not evidence of therapeutic activity.

PeTFlow couples peptide identity and conformation

A vocabulary of 1,024 residue-state tokens connects amino-acid identity with side-chain conformation for pocket-conditioned peptide generation. PeTFlow jointly models sequence, backbone and side-chain states.

On 221 test complexes, the supplied summary reports Cα RMSD improving from 4.437 Å with PepFlow to 3.213 Å, and DockQ rising from 0.379 to 0.424. These are computational recovery results; no experimental binding validation is described.

Biology AI Daily · Source post

kitAb favors interpretable, assay-specific antibody models

The open-source framework combines 59 physicochemical descriptors with automated feature selection and regression for individual developability endpoints. Across 33 endpoints, its reported mean Spearman correlation is around 0.36, matching PROPERMAB; descriptor computation was approximately 11 times faster in one CPU benchmark.

Performance varies substantially by assay. A separate analysis compares approximately 3.4 million natural paired antibodies with 706 Phase II-or-later therapeutics.

Biology AI Daily · Source post

CLARA makes active-learning strategy auditable

CLARA uses a language-model agent to distribute ligand-discovery candidates among exploitation, uncertainty and diversity queues. The supplied thread reports consistent improvement over random sampling and results matching the strongest fixed policy on some targets—not universal superiority.

The bounded role is the interesting part: the LLM provides a strategy layer inside a numerically controlled active-learning system.

Genomics & cell modeling

USM-AA models distant genomic windows without filling the gaps

USM-AA separates variant information from reference-genome position through alignment embeddings and coordinate-aware rotary positional encoding. This allows separated variant and transcription-start-site windows to be modeled without padding the intervening sequence.

The supplied summary reports leading eQTL results across nine cell types and enhancer–target prediction AUROC of 0.952 for the 1B model, alongside sparse somatic-variant cancer classification. These benchmark claims have not been independently verified here.

Biology AI Daily · Source post

Causal regulatory models need more than endpoint measurements

Anshul Kundaje emphasizes transcription-factor dosage, knockdown, combinatorial stoichiometry and temporal measurements as experimental-design choices for causal dynamic regulatory models. Cis-regulatory sequence logic could help narrow the search over transcription-factor combinations.

A related comment adds that perturbation order may matter even when final factor levels match. This is design commentary, not a supplied experimental result; the referenced paper and proposed active-learning strategy are unavailable in the excerpts.

BioMod turns biological modules into tokens

Described as a 50-million-parameter single-cell foundation model, BioMod uses 660 overlapping modules derived from curated pathways and data-driven gene programs. The representation sits between individual genes and whole cells, replacing thousands of gene-level inputs with compact module representations.

No quantitative evaluation is included in the supplied thread.

razoralign · Source post

DGP-LA adapts graph priors to cell state and perturbation

DGP-LA combines a cell-state-conditioned transition model with perturbation-conditioned sparse graph refinement. Gene-aligned target information propagates through the refined graph and enters latent representations as gated corrections.

The method targets single-cell perturbation prediction, but the supplied posts do not quantify its claimed generalization.

razoralign · Source post

scGSCA tests changes in coordinated gene activity

scGSCA fits gene-wise zero-inflated negative-binomial mixed models with subject-level random effects, then derives gene-set co-expression statistics from quantile residual correlations.

It supports differential co-expression analysis and an interaction test asking whether co-expression patterns between two cell types vary with sample phenotype—while accounting for donor structure.

AnnZarro opens atlas-scale relationships to browser exploration

The point-and-click viewer exposes per-cell, per-gene and pairwise quantities associated with AnnData objects, supporting slice-by-slice exploration when results are too large to display at once.

The thread reports a visualization containing all 95.6 million Tahoe-100M cells, but supplies no performance measurements.

AI methods, evaluation & safety

Can mutation predictions actually use structural context?

A partner-dependent benchmark keeps the protein, mutation site and substitution fixed while changing the binding partner. Success requires every outcome within a mutation group to be predicted correctly.

Across 60 groups and 126 observations, the supplied summary reports that the strongest direct LLM performs comparably to FoldX and Rosetta ddG on matched comparisons. Coordinate and partner-removal interventions probe reliance on structural evidence. The small benchmark and mechanistic errors caution against equating correct effect directions with general physical understanding.

Biology AI Daily · Source post

DRYLAB-Bench grounds biosecurity evaluation in existing measurements

The benchmark evaluates biological-design agents using empirical mutational measurements, rather than synthesizing new variants or relying on LLM judges. Its supplied summary describes 30 scenarios and 187,695 measurements.

Reported assay-based risk scores increase with iterative tool use, while safeguards are sensitive to request framing. These retrospective proxies are not demonstrations of real-world harmful capability; their value is in making risk and safeguard evaluation more empirically grounded.

Biology AI Daily · Source post

OpenMed tests when a care-gap system should abstain

A synthetic diabetes panel contains 12 patients: three each classified as met, open, not applicable and insufficient data. Unknown or conflicting inputs go to human review rather than outreach; an open gap requires complete, explicit evidence.

The demonstration uses simple native rules over record type, status and count—not CQL. It illustrates missing-data handling in decision support, not clinical validation.

Deutsch — Warum Berlin eine strengere EU-Handelspolitik gegen China unterstützt

Deutsche Welle · 13.10.2026 · https://www.dw.com/de/warum-berlin-eine-strengere-eu-handelspolitik-gegen-china-unterst%C3%BCtzt/a-79644973

Deutschland hat eine härtere Handelspolitik gegenüber China lange aus Sorge um seine Exporte gebremst. Der DW-Beitrag erklärt, warum Berlin inzwischen umsteuert: Chinesische Unternehmen konkurrieren zunehmend auch in Europas industriellen Schlüsselbranchen mit deutschen Herstellern. Besonders die Autoindustrie verliert Marktanteile, während günstigere Fahrzeuge aus chinesischer Produktion auf den europäischen Markt drängen. Damit wächst auch aus der Wirtschaft der Druck, handelspolitische Schutzmaßnahmen zu ergreifen.

Gemeinsam mit Frankreich fordert die Bundesregierung laut dem Bericht ein neues EU-Instrument, das bei anhaltend unfairen Handelspraktiken den Ausschluss eines Handelspartners vom Binnenmarkt ermöglichen soll. Zugleich arbeitet die EU an Maßnahmen zur Stärkung der Produktion in Europa. Doch auch Wirtschaftsverbände bezweifeln, dass die bisherigen Vorschläge die höheren europäischen Produktionskosten ausreichend ausgleichen können.

Der Artikel beleuchtet außerdem die Risiken einer Eskalation: China könnte den Export wichtiger Seltenerdmetalle und Magnete einschränken, auf die mehrere deutsche Industriezweige angewiesen sind. Dem steht Europas Bedeutung als Absatzmarkt für chinesische Waren gegenüber. Aus dieser gegenseitigen Abhängigkeit ergibt sich die zentrale Frage, wie die EU ihre Industrie schützen kann, ohne einen umfassenden Handelskrieg auszulösen.

Warum lesen: Der Beitrag macht verständlich, warum Deutschlands Exportinteressen nicht mehr automatisch für Zurückhaltung gegenüber China sprechen und welche Zielkonflikte daraus für die EU entstehen.

Français — Raphaël Sigal explore les liens entre naissance, lait et mémoire dans Au commencement, le lait

ActuaLitté · 14 octobre 2026 · Lire l’article

Comment une expérience de paternité peut-elle ouvrir une réflexion sur la mémoire collective ? ActuaLitté présente le prochain livre de Raphaël Sigal, Au commencement, le lait, annoncé chez Robert Laffont pour le 7 janvier 2027. La naissance de son fils et les difficultés liées à l’allaitement constituent le point de départ du texte : l’auteur s’interroge sur la place du père devant cette relation nourricière, puis élargit son regard à l’histoire humaine.

Cette présentation souligne les rapprochements opérés entre les premières pratiques de traite au Néolithique, la poésie de Paul Celan et les violences du XXe siècle. Le lait n’est donc pas seulement envisagé comme un aliment : il permet d’aborder la coexistence de la vie et de la mort, mais aussi ce que les parents lèguent, parfois sans le savoir, à leurs enfants. Le langage et la transmission familiale occupent ainsi une place centrale dans cet ouvrage, dont l’article annonce un extrait en avant-parution.

Pourquoi lire : Une courte découverte littéraire pour réfléchir, en français, aux liens entre expérience du corps, héritage familial et mémoire historique.

Vocabulaire utile

  • avant-parution — présentation ou diffusion d’un extrait d’ouvrage avant sa sortie en librairie.
  • traite — opération consistant à recueillir le lait d’un animal d’élevage.
  • Néolithique — période de la préhistoire marquée notamment par le développement de l’agriculture et de l’élevage.

AUTOMATON · 2026-10-13 · https://automaton-media.com/articles/newsjp/triple-a-minesweeper-20261013-473202/

個人開発者のMike Lacher氏が、定番の地雷探しゲームを大予算の大型作品風に仕立てた『Triple-A Minesweeper』を無料公開した。起動すると企業やソフトウェアのロゴが次々に表示され、さらに主人公が気絶から目覚める演出や、通信相手からの音声指示が続く。静かに数字を読み解くはずの遊びに過剰な演出を重ね、大作ゲームの慣習を笑いの対象にしている。

盤面には正解へ誘導する黄色いマーカーまであり、そのとおりに操作すれば推理せずにクリアできる。地雷を踏んでもダメージ表示が出るだけで、ゲームオーバーにはならない。記事は、こうした仕組みを「親切過ぎるガイド」への皮肉として紹介し、絶え間ない音声指示などに心当たりのあるゲームファンから共感が寄せられていると伝える。便利な案内と、自分で考える楽しさの境目を考えさせる短編だ。

中文 — 中国为何愿意就混合动力汽车 限制对欧洲的出口？

RFI 中文 · 2026-10-14 · 原文链接

法广报道，中国与欧盟就混合动力汽车出口达成协议，中方承诺在四年内将对欧洲的出口量削减一半以上，但具体执行机制仍不清楚。文章回顾了这一争议的背景：欧盟针对中国制造电动汽车的关税措施未涵盖混合动力汽车，中国车企随后扩大了在欧洲这一细分市场的份额，使汽车竞争成为中欧贸易关系中的突出议题。

文章将双方选择谈判的原因归结为各自的产业压力。欧洲车企面临竞争，法国与德国领导人要求加强市场保护；但欧洲也担心贸易冲突影响关键原材料供应。另一方面，中国汽车市场增长减弱、品牌竞争激烈，而美国的高关税又限制了出口空间。作者据此分析，保住欧洲销售渠道、避免更大规模的贸易战，是中方愿意作出让步的重要考量。

为什么读： 这篇文章把中国汽车出口、德法产业利益与欧盟贸易政策联系起来，适合练习阅读带有因果分析的中文经济报道。

词汇笔记

  • 混合动力汽车 (hùnhé dònglì qìchē) — 同时使用内燃机与电动机等动力来源的汽车。
  • 贸易逆差 (màoyì nìchā) — 在一定时期内，进口总额超过出口总额的情况。
  • 关税 (guānshuì) — 商品进出关境时由海关依法征收的税，可影响进口商品的价格与竞争力。
