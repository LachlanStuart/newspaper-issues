Top story

Nesso-1 speeds up open-source protein–ligand affinity prediction

Nesso-1 replaces atomistic diffusion with coarse-grained cofolding that models relationships between ligand atoms and protein residue centers. It predicts affinity in roughly one second on a single GPU and is reported to run more than ten times faster than Boltz-2 while outperforming it on several public benchmarks and 25 internal biochemical assays.

The authors also offer an unusually candid warning about evaluation: many public affinity benchmarks are largely in-distribution, while molecular weight remains a competitive baseline on difficult zero-shot tests. Fast virtual screening is useful, but reliable generalization to unfamiliar chemistry and targets remains unresolved.
