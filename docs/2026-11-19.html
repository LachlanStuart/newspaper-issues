Genomics & single-cell biology

Easy synthetic variants still challenge Enformer fine-tuning

LAMP introduces artificial single-nucleotide variants affecting transcription-factor motifs into personal-genome datasets, giving them large, consistent causal effects on expression. Even with these deliberately easy-to-learn perturbations, the reported experiments find that fine-tuning Enformer underperforms on HOGP. The study argues that difficulties adapting sequence-to-function models extend beyond the complexity of natural genetic variation.

Internal priming flagged in 10x 3′ capture

A Goyal lab preprint highlighted by Anshul Kundaje reports that oligo(dT) in 10x 3′ capture primes at internal A-rich stretches approximately 80 times more often than at the true poly(A) tail—a measurement artifact relevant to interpreting single-cell transcriptomic data.

GatorAnchor makes cross-modal disagreement explicit

GatorAnchor learns modality-specific Gaussian representations from graph-augmented single-cell multiomics features and fuses them through a precision-weighted product of experts. Disagreement between modalities contributes to latent dispersion, while modality reliability and neighborhood evidence guide refinement of cell–cell relationships.

TMCells jointly learns topics and metacells

TMCells represents metacells as distribution centers in a latent topic space with a Gaussian-mixture prior. It jointly learns interpretable topics and probabilistic cell partitions, accounting for continuity and uncertainty in cell-state transitions. The source reports improvements in differential expression, rare-cell identification and trajectory inference while preserving biological heterogeneity.

TMCells research post

STRUCT searches raw reads for conserved RNA structures

STRUCT—Statistical Testing of RNA Units with Covariation Traits—tests RNA covariation directly in sequencing reads, bypassing assembly and multiple-sequence alignment. It examines variable sequences linked to a shared fixed sequence to infer potential compensatory mutations in stem-loops and detect apparent negative selection against structure-disrupting variants.

Claude contact prediction shows prompt-dependent reproducibility

Sergey Ovchinnikov reports reconstructing the ubiquitin fold from contacts predicted by Claude Fable 5.2, then finding that the original simple prompt did not reliably reproduce the result across runs. After extracting a more detailed prompt from the successful session, he reports repeating runs three times across three Claude models, with Fable 5.2 appearing stronger on contact prediction.

He emphasizes that the tests involved easy proteins and that turning a good contact map into a structure is the less informative step. His proposed stronger test is prediction on proteins absent from the PDB and difficult for AlphaFold.

VReceptor tests peptide-design strategies with synthetic assays

VReceptor generates sequence-dependent synthetic peptide affinities using position-specific pharmacophore-like weights, physicochemical substitution distances and assay noise. In an equal-budget simulation with 589 peptides, triple-mutant sampling yielded lower prediction error than a complete single-mutant scan.

A roughly six-parameter model fitted to experimental PrRP–GPR10 data reached a test Pearson correlation of about 0.83. The additive formulation omits explicit epistasis and structure, and performance degraded on NPFFR1/NPFFR2.

IMPRESS reallocates computation during molecular design

IMPRESS replaces fixed design cascades with asynchronous, stateful workflows that stop, resample or branch according to intermediate results. Demonstrations span discontinuous enzyme active sites, small-molecule binders and PDZ peptide-binder redesign.

For five-island enzyme motifs, it reportedly found high-quality computational solutions in more than 40% of cases where the nonadaptive reference found none under a matched-budget analysis. Comparisons are entirely in silico, and steering and evaluation often use the same metrics; the principal claim is better exploration and control of generated ensembles.

IMPRESS research summary

EnzRef separates reactant and product signals

EnzRef predicts log10 kcat using a frozen protein language model and 1.72 million trainable parameters. It conditions residue representations separately on reactants and products, subtracts an enzyme-specific reference and combines shared and role-dependent responses.

The study reports source-held-out R² of 0.614 versus 0.553 for MCKcat, and enzyme-unseen transfer R² of 0.310 versus 0.259. It also reports better within-enzyme and within-reaction activity comparisons, with approximately 3.14× faster inference using cached features.

EnzRef research summary

Remote homology search without structures at inference

S-PLM-TM distills structural information into sequence embeddings through cross-modal flow matching and contrastive learning, then calibrates similarity against TM-align scores. It supports remote-homology retrieval and residue-level alignment without requiring structures at inference.

The source reports leading sequence-based retrieval performance and strong functional consistency among retrieved neighbors. Searching 957 queries against 430,140 pre-embedded Swiss-Prot targets took 8.6 seconds on one A100. An optional enzyme-classification head supports catalytic-function retrieval.

OpenMed fine-tune lifts procedure-extraction F1 to 48.0

OpenMed reports that GLiNER2.5 ProceduresTests Small improved F1 from 12.6 to 48.0 on 200 held-out abstracts after fine-tuning on 1,000 MedMentions abstracts with three labels. The model is Apache-2.0 licensed.

Laboratory procedures remain a weakness: F1 is 28.9, with about one in four found. The model also missed “maintenance hemodialysis” in a demonstration and has not been tested on hospital notes.

Français — France: l'Assurance maladie adresse un courrier aux jeunes de 29 ans pour les sensibiliser à la fertilité

RFI · 16 novembre 2026 · Lire l’article

L’Assurance maladie lance une campagne d’information destinée aux Français de 29 ans, qui doivent recevoir un courrier sur leur compte Ameli. Le message aborde aussi bien la contraception que les difficultés à concevoir, la diminution de la fertilité avec l’âge et la possibilité de conserver ses gamètes pour un projet parental plus tardif. Initialement prévu pour l’été, cet envoi avait été repoussé après des critiques visant le ton de sa première version, jugé intrusif et infantilisant.

Selon RFI, la nouvelle formulation satisfait davantage les associations, qui demandent une meilleure information sur la santé reproductive. Virginie Rio, cofondatrice de BAMP, souligne notamment le décalage entre les attentes de certaines personnes et les difficultés qu’elles découvrent lorsqu’elles souhaitent avoir un enfant. Cette initiative fait partie d’un plan de lutte contre l’infertilité comprenant également le déploiement de nouveaux centres de conservation des ovocytes, afin d’améliorer l’accès à cette démarche et de raccourcir les délais d’attente.

Pourquoi lire : Un article accessible pour explorer les tensions entre prévention sanitaire, choix personnels et communication publique autour de la fertilité.

Vocabulaire utile

  • gamètes — cellules reproductrices : spermatozoïdes et ovocytes.
  • conservation des ovocytes — congélation et stockage de cellules reproductrices féminines en vue d’une utilisation ultérieure.
  • insémination — technique d’aide à la procréation consistant à introduire des spermatozoïdes dans l’appareil reproducteur féminin.

日本語 — パスワードより安全なはずが……パスキー対応103サイトを検証→全てに脆弱性　独大学の調査結果

ITmedia NEWS · 2026-11-16 · https://www.itmedia.co.jp/news/article/2611/16/2000003199/

ドイツのルール大学ボーフムなどの研究チームが、パスキーに対応するウェブサイトの普及状況と安全性を調べた。パスキーは、公開鍵暗号と端末の生体認証やPINを利用するログイン技術だ。しかし、登録できた208サイトの基本設定を調べると、パスキーを追加する際に追加の本人確認を求めないサイトが66％を占め、削除機能や複数登録への対応にも不備があった。

さらに103サイトを対象に15種類の攻撃手法で検証したところ、認証技術「WebAuthn」が求める全てのセキュリティ基準を満たすサイトはなかった。18サイトで緊急レベル、53サイトで重要レベルの脆弱性が見つかり、暗号署名を検証していない例も確認された。記事が紹介するのは、パスキーという仕組みそのものだけでなく、各サイトでの実装や運用が安全性を左右するという問題だ。

読む理由： ドイツ発の研究を通して、身近なログイン技術の安全性と、導入するだけでは防げないリスクを具体的に学べる。

中文 — 欧委会初步认定Temu违反《数字服务法》：未能降低平台出现非法产品风险

RFI 中文 · 2026-11-17 · 原文链接

据法广报道，欧盟委员会初步认定，跨境电商平台Temu没有充分履行《数字服务法》规定的风险防范义务。调查指出，平台对产品测试报告的要求和核查不足，也没有在实物抽检中优先关注玩具、电子产品和化妆品等高风险商品。问题不仅在于商品信息是否符合平台规则，更在于商品本身是否符合欧盟安全要求。

报道还介绍了审核与处罚机制中的漏洞：人工审核人员没有随商品数量增长而相应增加，处罚又主要针对单个店铺，而不是背后的商家。因此，一家店铺被关闭后，同一商家仍可能通过其他店铺继续销售。欧委会认为，这种做法削弱了处罚的威慑作用。

目前这仍是初步认定，并非最终裁定。Temu可以查阅调查文件并提交书面回应；如果认定最终得到确认，平台可能面临最高相当于全球年度总营业额6%的罚款，并须采取整改措施。

为什么读： 这篇报道把中国跨境电商、欧盟数字监管和日常网购安全联系起来，适合练习区分调查结论、法律义务与潜在处罚。

词汇笔记

  • 抗辩权 (kàng biàn quán) — 当事人针对有关指控或认定提出事实、理由和证据，为自己辩护的权利。
  • 整改措施 (zhěng gǎi cuò shī) — 为纠正违规行为、使经营活动符合规定而采取的具体行动。
