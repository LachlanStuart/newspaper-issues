CREMAP tests whether chromatin accessibility and gene expression still vary together after accounting for sequencing depth and other shared sources of variation. The associations suggest candidate regulatory links without establishing causality.

CyteArc analyzes remote cell atlases on standard CPUs

CyteArc processes remote atlases approaching 100 million cells in place. It was faster than the workflows it was compared with and used the least memory at 10 million cells and above. Source data stay read-only, and results remain linked to their inputs and settings.

interpreTFM compares sparse features extracted from scGPT, Geneformer, and C2S-scale with the models’ raw embeddings. It tests both against held-out gene annotations and asks whether the extracted features recover tissue organization and boundaries between cells.

Cell annotation improves when the whole batch counts

In a task requiring one-to-one label assignments, scOPD with Qwen3-8B labeled 44.49% of batches entirely correctly, compared with 24.52% for fine-tuning alone. Per-cell accuracy was 81.71%, illustrating how a cell-level score can mask batch-level errors.

scPriorDiff uses known regulatory links and genomic proximity to help a diffusion model infer RNA expression or chromatin accessibility when one of those measurements is missing.

In ModFormer’s evaluation of naturally occurring ADAR editing, RNA structure mattered more for known duplexes, while sequence mattered more in transcriptome-wide predictions. Adapting the model to one sequencing library made it less effective on others.

Biomedicine & protein modeling

Dual-target therapeutic peptides show preclinical activity

A Nature study reports that six designed peptides acting on GLP-1R and GCGR were more potent than the corresponding natural agonists in receptor assays. A separate peptide targeting PD-1 and CTLA-4 reduced tumor volume by 94.2% versus control in mice carrying human immune checkpoints. The findings do not establish efficacy or tolerability in patients.

Inherited IL7 variation studied as a marker of immunotherapy toxicity

A study in the Journal for ImmunoTherapy of Cancer examines whether inherited variation in IL7 can help predict adverse effects from immune checkpoint inhibitors. The focus is treatment safety, not tumor response.

Cofolding models struggle with cyclic peptides

In the PRETZEL benchmark of 285 cyclic peptide–protein complexes, none of four models placed more than about 20% of predicted poses within 2 Å of the reference. Even the best model produced valid distances for every inter-residue bond in only 8.1% of predictions.

AlloGator tests predictions of long-range effects in proteins

Français — Notre avis sur « GTA VI » après soixante heures de jeu : entre vertige urbain et romance sous tension

Le Monde · 26 novembre 2026 · Lire l’article

Après soixante heures de jeu, Le Monde revient sur ce qui fait la force de GTA VI : une ville fictive inspirée de Miami où le moindre détour peut se transformer en petite histoire. La critique s’ouvre sur un vol de voiture qui tourne à l’absurde quand un influenceur caché dans le coffre croit participer à une vidéo entre amis. La scène passe de la satire des réseaux sociaux à la course-poursuite.

Le test souligne aussi le soin apporté aux décors, à la lumière et aux reflets. Ces détails donnent envie de quitter l’itinéraire prévu pour explorer Vice City. Le bilan est toutefois nuancé : si la ville tient ses promesses, la conclusion de l’histoire manque de rythme. La suite de l’article est réservée aux abonnés.

Pourquoi lire : Pour comprendre comment un jeu vidéo peut raconter des histoires en dehors de ses missions principales.

Vocabulaire utile

  • monde ouvert — espace de jeu que l’on peut explorer assez librement, sans suivre un parcours imposé.
  • microhistoire — bref récit qui se déroule en marge de l’intrigue principale.
  • trame principale — suite d’événements qui structure le récit central d’un jeu.

中文 — 台湾选举漫游录：在花莲丰滨问“为什么原乡那么蓝？”

端传媒 · 2026-11-27 · 原文（会员限定）

十月中旬，记者在花莲县丰滨乡的港口村拍摄乡长候选人的海报，一位当地居民上前询问来意。记者说想了解丰滨的选举为什么那么“蓝”，对方却反问，这跟他们有什么关系。

报道从这段对话切入：外来者想用蓝绿阵营理解地方选举，当地人却不一定接受这样的提问。导语里还有一句反问，把焦点从支持哪个政党转向另一件事：如果自己始终是被统治的一方，换谁执政又有多大区别？

为什么读： 从一次街头碰面，看台湾选举中常见的蓝绿标签如何遭遇地方居民的反问。

词汇笔记

  • 原乡 (yuánxiāng) — 在台湾语境中，常指原住民族传统聚居的地区。
  • 泛蓝 (fànlán) — 以中国国民党为主的政治阵营；标题里的“蓝”指向这一政治倾向。
  • 乡长 (xiāngzhǎng) — 台湾乡级地方自治行政首长，由当地选民选出。
