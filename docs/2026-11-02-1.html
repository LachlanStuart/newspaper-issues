Genomics & cells

Cross-species benchmarks expose the importance of gene coverage

A study of zero-shot transfer to Dictyostelium and C. elegans compares Geneformer, scGPT and UCE. UCE’s protein-derived representations cover about 97% of measured genes, compared with roughly 17–18% retained through ortholog mapping for human-vocabulary models. UCE-4L leads five of six headline annotation tasks—but coverage-matched controls largely remove its Dictyostelium cell-type advantage.

PCA and scVI remain competitive on some endpoints. The result is a reminder that gene coverage, preprocessing and checkpoint depth can matter as much as the model when interpreting a leaderboard.

EMBER predicts perturbations with contextual gene embeddings

EMBER combines interaction-guided contrastive learning with masked expression reconstruction on a single-cell corpus curated for cell-type diversity. A frozen pretrained encoder supplies contextual gene embeddings to lightweight probes that predict individual and combined perturbation effects. The supplied thread describes the method but provides no quantitative benchmark results.

FiberBrowser brings different chromatin assays into a shared view

FiberBrowser and FiberHMM translate four footprinting assay/platform combinations into common annotations for nucleosomes, accessible DNA and short footprints. Modeling each assay’s informative sites helps distinguish genuine protection from a lack of measurement opportunity. The browser also supports haplotype splitting, methylation overlays and inspection of chromatin across inserted sequences.

A cross-assay classification demonstration correctly identified all 12 tested scDAF-seq cells without retraining: encouraging transfer evidence, but a small test.

Evo 2 enhancer-design follow-up adds experimental evidence

A follow-up account describes barcoded MPRA tests in three human cell lines, where generated enhancers showed target-biased activity and reinforcement learning improved measured specificity in some settings. In a mouse experiment, seven of nine designs drove detectable expression. Two designs reported 73% and 95% specificity among YFP-positive cells for layer 5 extratelencephalic neurons.

Attention-head ablation also supports a role for induction-like mechanisms. These limited experimental validations should be distinguished from broader predicted improvements.

Molecules & therapeutics

AlphaFold 3 TCR-specificity scores depend strongly on the assay

A study uses peptide–TCR interface uncertainty from AlphaFold 3 as a specificity score, with homology filtering and antigen-study-level evaluation. Reported median AUCs reach approximately 0.93–0.94 for binding-assay entries, but fall to 0.61 for class I and 0.52 for class II multimer-derived assignments.

Held-out tests report median AUCs of 0.92 for a small post-cutoff class I structural set and 0.81 across eight unseen tuberculosis antigens. The contrast makes assay provenance and affinity regime essential context for claims of generalization.

Study summary · BiologyAIDaily

PSICHIC+ puts uncertainty into large-scale bioactivity prediction

Trained on approximately 82 million records, PSICHIC+ predicts affinity distributions from protein sequences and ligand SMILES. It combines exact measurements, thresholded assay evidence and synthetic negatives, with source-aware regularization intended to curb overconfidence on noisier data.

Across 16 target assays, the supplied account reports a mean correlation around 0.52 at approximately 1.34 milliseconds per pair on an A100. A confidence-weighted ensemble with Boltz-2 reaches about 0.68. Screening and iterative acquisition results are computational benchmarks and simulations, not prospective wet-lab validation.

Model and benchmark summary · BiologyAIDaily

ChefNMR2 reconstructs molecular connectivity from one-dimensional NMR

ChefNMR2 uses atomic flow matching to generate candidate structures from a molecular formula and proton/carbon NMR peak lists. Across five test sets totaling 2,031 molecules, the account reports 82.3% top-1 planar-structure accuracy and an approximately 1.3-percentage-point calibration error.

An agent-assisted extension adds spectral checks and hypothesis testing for four newly elucidated natural products. The method requires a known molecular formula and does not resolve stereochemistry.

Method summary · BiologyAIDaily

SPARC designs light-controlled protein switches

De novo retinal-based SPARC switches couple illumination to conformational change and peptide binding. Optimized designs reportedly exceed 30-fold activation in split-luciferase assays, with crystallography, spectroscopy and light-activated NMR supporting the mechanism. Cellular demonstrations include light-dependent binding and FGFR1-linked calcium signaling.

Those demonstrations required retinal supplementation and a covalent locking strategy; performance does not yet match mature optogenetic tools.

Switch-design summary · BiologyAIDaily

SAbDab2 strengthens antibody benchmark curation

An October 2026 ML-ready subset contains 16,415 curated antibody instances. Sequence-defined identifiers group the same antibody across structures and bound states, while standardized splits address both antibody similarity and antibody–antigen similarity. Updated parsing supports mmCIF, scFv and other engineered formats, and ANARCII expands antibody identification.

These follow-up details matter for redundancy control and more reproducible evaluation of therapeutic-antibody models.

TACFormer generates whole PROTACs, with an unseen-target gap

TACFormer conditions a molecular decoder on ESM-2 target embeddings, using 30,053 molecule–target pairs across 408 proteins. Unlike linker-only approaches, it generates complete PROTAC molecules. Seen-target outputs show high validity and target-associated chemical-neighborhood purity, but five-neighbor purity drops to about 30.5% on four unseen targets.

These metrics measure chemical distribution matching—not demonstrated binding or protein degradation.

Model summary · BiologyAIDaily

Little Protein Tiger uses fixed stages to constrain binder-design agents

LPT combines a curated literature database, deterministic interface analysis and fixed-stage workflows for disease-relevant protein interactions. Three campaigns report computational gate-passing rates of approximately 9.6–17.9%, followed by cross-model refolding of top candidates.

Reported LLM API costs are below $0.50 per campaign, separate from 11–34 hours of local GPU computation. No experimental binding or functional validation is described; agreement among structure predictors is not proof of biological activity.

OpenMed compares disease extraction under a common protocol

After training on NCBI Disease only, OpenMed reports BC5CDR F1 scores on 500 PubMed abstracts using a shared threshold of 0.5:

  • GLiNER2.5 Base: 67.0 F1; 194 million parameters.
  • GLiFormer Base: 64.1 F1; 264 million parameters.
  • GLiFormer Large: 69.0 F1; 576 million parameters.

All three are described as Apache-2.0 and CPU-compatible. An accompanying synthetic-note demonstration uses model-specific default thresholds and should not be confused with this benchmark.

Français — Mouvement lycéen : les incertitudes de la rentrée scolaire alors que la jeunesse mobilisée veut « maintenir la pression »

Le Monde · 1er novembre 2026 · Lire l’article

La reprise des cours du 2 novembre pose une question qui dépasse le calendrier scolaire : les lycéens vont-ils reprendre les blocages après les vacances de la Toussaint ? Le mouvement, lancé à la mi-septembre, était encore actif à la fermeture des établissements. Malgré les engagements du gouvernement, les organisations lycéennes et étudiantes appellent à de nouvelles actions. Mais les préoccupations liées au baccalauréat et à Parcoursup rendent certains élèves hésitants, selon la présentation de l’article.

Pendant les vacances, la contestation a changé d’échelle : elle a rejoint une mobilisation plus large contre la vie chère et pour les services publics, avec des manifestations les 17, 24 et 31 octobre. L’enjeu de la rentrée est donc de savoir comment cette dynamique se traduira devant les établissements. L’introduction accessible met en évidence l’incertitude partagée par les élèves, les directions et les ministères ; la suite de cet article d’Eléa Pommiers est réservée aux abonnés. Cet aperçu repose sur cette introduction et sur la présentation publiée en une.

Pourquoi lire : Pour comprendre les tensions entre engagement collectif, revendications sociales et contraintes scolaires dans la jeunesse française.

Vocabulaire utile

  • blocus — action collective qui empêche l’accès à un établissement pour soutenir une revendication.
  • Parcoursup — plateforme nationale de candidature à de nombreuses formations de l’enseignement supérieur en France.
  • mobilisation — rassemblement et mise en action de personnes autour d’une cause ou de revendications communes.

中文 — 朝鲜“精英”黑客组织是谁？为何成为币安63亿美元盗窃案的头号嫌疑者？

BBC 中文 · 2026-10-31 · https://www.bbc.com/zhongwen/articles/cqe8rpl8wjd0o/simp

据BBC报道，区块链分析公司TRM Labs和Chainalysis认为，朝鲜黑客组织“拉撒路集团”很可能涉及币安63亿美元加密货币盗窃案。文章借此梳理朝鲜被指参与的网络攻击，从索尼影业电邮外泄到加密货币交易所失窃，解释网络犯罪如何被怀疑成为其获取资金的渠道。朝鲜政府长期否认有关指控，币安也未直接回应BBC关于朝鲜是否涉案的查询。

这次事件的技术焦点是连接互联网的“热钱包”。TRM认为，攻击者很可能取得了钱包签署系统的存取权限，进而改变资金转移方式。币安最初怀疑工程师的电脑遭入侵，随后承认发现更多受影响环节；公司发言人于10月30日表示，约八成被盗资产已得到保全，其余资产去向仍不明。

文章还讨论人工智能可能如何帮助攻击者提高自动化能力，以及追踪被盗资产面临的困难。不过，人工智能是否参与本案仍属推测，不能与已确认的调查发现混为一谈。

为什么读： 这篇报道把网络安全、数字金融与国际政治联系起来，也适合练习辨别中文新闻中的指控、推测与确认事实。
