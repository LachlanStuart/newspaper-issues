Sesame generates 3D molecules using protein-pocket and fragment density-map conditioning

X Bio Research

Sesame is a diffusion-based 3D molecular generator for de novo ligand design and fragment-conditioned lead optimization. Protein pockets and partial ligands are represented as continuous spatial density maps, with a pairformer module injecting sampled density features into atom and pair representations. The method combines coordinate diffusion with categorical atom and bond diffusion and reports improved validity after trajectory finetuning.

X Bio Research
