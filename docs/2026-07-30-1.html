Catalytic-antibody pipeline reveals limits in preserving active-site geometry

A preprint attempts to transplant experimentally supported enzyme active-site geometry into antibody CDR loops, then rebuild and redesign the scaffold with RFdiffusion3, LigandMPNN, and RoseTTAFold3. VHH scaffolds tolerated grafting better than two-chain Fv scaffolds, but refolding generally failed to recover the full non-covalent catalytic network—even after a missing covalent-bond specification was corrected. The negative results expose the brittleness of current generative protein-design systems when exact catalytic microenvironments matter.

HydrAffinity predicts protein–ligand affinity without docking

HydrAffinity is a preparation-free affinity model intended as a fast virtual-screening prefilter. It combines pretrained ligand and protein representations through cascaded sparse mixture-of-experts modules and reports CASF-2016 accuracy competitive with interaction-based methods. Zero-shot screening is strong on DUDE-Z and LIT-PCBA, although performance deteriorates under extreme shifts in charge distribution.

Vilya-2 models chemically diverse peptide interfaces

Vilya-2 is an all-atom diffusion Transformer for local protein–ligand interface modeling. It uses a unified atomic graph rather than residue tokens, molecule-type labels, or multiple-sequence alignments. On Riptides, a post-2023 benchmark enriched for non-canonical cyclic peptides, it reports substantially higher interface recovery than template-assisted Boltz-2. Diverse sampling, ligand-focused confidence calibration, and physical-validity filtering are central to the approach.

TriGlue jointly generates molecular glues and ternary-complex geometry

TriGlue frames molecular-glue discovery as interface creation. Given an E3 ligase and an unbound target, it predicts a protein–protein interface and jointly generates a glue molecule and the rigid-body transformation needed to assemble the ternary complex. Its equivariant architecture combines virtual interface points with interface-conditioned flow matching. Reported ligand placement improves on several pocket-conditioned baselines, though absolute docking quality remains modest.

Persistent Manifold Learning spans ligand and protein interfaces

This method represents binding interfaces with multiscale atomic-density level sets and graph-Laplacian descriptors, then combines those geometric and topological features with ESM-2 and ChemBERTa embeddings. A gradient-boosted predictor reports competitive affinity results for both metalloprotein–ligand complexes and protein–protein interactions. Its notable feature is a single interpretable representation that works across compact metal sites and broad protein interfaces.

A new website compares 15 antibody–antigen structure-prediction variants on a shared set of targets released from September 2024 onward.
