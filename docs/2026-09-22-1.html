How small can a useful scaling-law experiment be?

A FAIR and NYU study examines how far scaling-law experiments can be reduced without losing their fit or predictive value. The work focuses on whether cheaper preliminary runs can still provide dependable guidance for larger language-model experiments. The discussion links to the full paper.

Speech recognition

Parakeet Redux compresses a speech model to ternary weights

Parakeet Redux reduces NVIDIA’s Parakeet speech-to-text model from 1.2 GB to 178 MB through ternary quantization and a post-training recovery pipeline. Its release reports CPU inference at 113 times real time, improved results over the base model on the 25-language FLEURS benchmark, and English performance within 0.3 WER. Tables and examples appear in the linked model card.

RL theory

Score centering as a straight-through estimator

A concise derivation writes the logit-space policy score as e_a − p and its expectation under sampling distribution q as q − p, yielding the centered score e_a − q. This produces the straight-through-estimator interpretation z_p + sg(z_q − z_p) without requiring a linear policy.

Protein design

Proteína-Complexa searches latent space for biomolecular interactions

Proteína-Complexa jointly generates protein sequence and structure, then uses reward-guided inference-time search to improve molecular interfaces. Experimental work includes a crystallized 800-residue design, binders against PDGFR, PD-L1 and Nipah virus glycoprotein, and multiplexed screening of more than one million sequences across 127 targets. The framework also produced compact binders able to distinguish blood-group glycans.

Generative models · Wet-lab validation · Glycobiology · Drug discovery

Virtual screening

DrugCLIP analysis tests generalization and interpretability

DrugCLIP’s pocket embeddings strongly reflect binding-site geometry, while its ligand embeddings capture a pocket-aware form of chemical similarity. Evaluations designed to remove leakage retain substantial enrichment on novel proteins and chemistry, though performance declines with conformational changes, predicted pockets, and mismatched residue sets.

Contrastive learning · Evaluation · Molecular embeddings

Virtual cells

CELL-FM connects protein sequences with microscopy

CELL-FM is a bidirectional sequence–image model: it can generate fluorescence microscopy from protein sequence and cellular context, or design sequences from image phenotypes. Trained on HPA, OpenCell and CondenSeq collections, it recovered known localization signals and generated NLS and NES motifs that were validated in cells. Applications also include morphology-controlled protein atlases and virtual mutagenesis of phase-separating proteins.

Microscopy · Protein design · Cell biology · Generative modeling

RNA design

RNA-MDLM generates and inpaints non-coding RNA

RNA-MDLM applies masked discrete diffusion to type-conditioned non-coding RNA generation and sequence inpainting. It introduces language-model-derived RNA-type prototypes, guidance for severe class imbalance, and the REPAINT GAMES reconstruction suite. The results highlight a tension between structural fidelity and novelty—and show how redundant datasets can reward copying while making outputs appear realistic.

Diffusion models · Synthetic biology · Evaluation

Viral surveillance

Viral Evolution Simulator forecasts mutation fitness

A diffusion model trained on viral protein sequences scores mutations through reconstruction-based likelihood ratios. Reported results improve on immune-escape and receptor-binding benchmarks, recover established influenza antigenic-drift sites, and rank mutations that subsequently rose after the model’s temporal training cutoff. An H5 analysis also prioritizes mutations associated with human-type receptor binding.

Influenza · Mutation fitness · Genomic surveillance

Gene regulation

scMGFGRN combines transcriptomes, function, and sequence

scMGFGRN infers gene-regulatory networks by combining single-cell transcriptomes with functional gene hierarchies and gene-sequence representations from a pretrained DNA language model. Its architecture uses denoising autoencoders, graph-attention feature extraction, and gated multi-head attention.

Single-cell data · Regulatory networks · DNA language models

Experimental prioritization

VCCV uses cellular evidence to triage drug–target predictions

Virtual-to-Cellular Corroboration for Validation is a model-agnostic layer for pretrained drug–target interaction predictors. It updates calibrated predictions with context-matched perturbational transcriptomics, can abstain through an empirical warning branch, and converts unresolved near-ties into compact gene panels for follow-up measurement.

Transcriptomics · Uncertainty · Experimental design

Interpretability

MESIC gives gene-expression embeddings sparse semantic components

MESIC compresses otherwise opaque semantic gene embeddings into a small collection of sparse components. Each component is concentrated on a limited gene set and can be explained using the corresponding gene summaries and annotations.

Gene expression · Representation learning · Interpretability

Deutsch — Nachfolger von Windows: Staatskanzlei Kiel stellt auf Linux um

heise online · 21.09.2026 · Artikel öffnen

Die Staatskanzlei Schleswig-Holsteins hat ihre Arbeitsplätze weitgehend von Microsoft Windows auf Linux umgestellt. Mehr als 200 Beschäftigte nutzen inzwischen das freie Betriebssystem; nur rund 20 Rechner bleiben wegen spezieller Fachanwendungen vorerst bei Windows. Schulungen, Lernvideos, interne Foren und technische Unterstützung sollen den Übergang erleichtern.

Das Projekt dient als Vorbild für die weitere Umstellung der Landesverwaltung. Schleswig-Holstein hat bereits LibreOffice, Open-Xchange und OpenTalk eingeführt; als Nächstes sind unter anderem Nextcloud und weitere quelloffene Dienste vorgesehen. Ziel ist es, die Abhängigkeit von einzelnen Softwareherstellern zu verringern und die Kontrolle über Daten und digitale Infrastruktur zu stärken.

Warum lesen: Der Artikel zeigt an einem konkreten deutschen Beispiel, wie digitale Souveränität und Open Source in der öffentlichen Verwaltung praktisch umgesetzt werden.

Français — Bungie annule la mort de Destiny 2

Numerama · 22 septembre 2026 · Lire l’article

Quelques mois après avoir laissé entendre que Destiny 2 approchait de sa fin, Bungie fait marche arrière. Le studio affirme que la franchise reste essentielle à son avenir et promet de lui consacrer de nouveau du temps et des ressources. Cette annonce intervient après une forte mobilisation des joueurs, mais aussi dans un contexte difficile pour l’entreprise, marquée par des licenciements et par les résultats décevants de Marathon.

Bungie prévoit notamment de réintroduire dans Destiny 2 des campagnes, destinations et raids supprimés au fil des années. L’opération sera complexe : les anciens contenus devront être adaptés aux systèmes techniques, à l’éclairage, aux ennemis et aux normes de jouabilité actuels. Aucun Destiny 3 ni nouvelle extension n’est encore annoncé, mais le studio présente ce chantier comme la première étape d’un engagement à long terme envers la saga et sa communauté.

中文 — “弱化英语”为什么成为中国社会的周期性话题？

端传媒 · 2026-09-22 · 阅读文章

文章以中国社会反复出现的“弱化英语”讨论为切口，关注英语教育为何不只是一项课程安排，还会周期性地演变为公共议题。标题所提出的问题，把外语学习与教育政策、社会心态及中国如何理解自身与外部世界的关系联系起来。

导语认为，新一代中国人正变得更不愿意“与世界接轨”。这一判断值得结合文章进一步辨析：英语地位的变化究竟源于教学负担、实用价值的重新评估，还是更广泛的社会与政治氛围。正文仅限会员阅读，但公开标题和导语已清楚呈现文章的核心讨论方向。

为什么读： 从语言教育观察中国社会对全球化与外部世界态度的变化，也与语言学习者自身密切相关。

词汇笔记

  • 弱化 (ruòhuà) — 降低某事物的重要性、地位或影响力。
  • 与世界接轨 (yǔ shìjiè jiēguǐ) — 与国际通行的制度、知识或交流方式建立联系。
  • 周期性话题 (zhōuqīxìng huàtí) — 每隔一段时间便会重新出现并引发讨论的话题。
