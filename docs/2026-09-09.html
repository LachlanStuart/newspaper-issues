Genomics and cellular systems

AlphaGenome Atlas maps all nine billion possible human SNVs

The roughly one-petabyte resource contains predicted molecular effects for every possible single-nucleotide substitution in the human genome. Its Variant Impact score combines regulatory predictions from AlphaGenome with protein-altering predictions from AlphaMissense. The breadth is notable, though experimental validation remains the bottleneck.

DECANT separates drug mechanism from experimental context

DECANT learns from matched treated and control single-cell profiles. It factorizes perturbational signals into a representation designed to remain stable across cell line, dose, and treatment time, plus a context-dependent residual for predicting gene- and program-level responses.

Botanic-1 applies long-context genomic models to plants

Botanic-1 comprises 318-million- to 3.2-billion-parameter models trained across 320 plant genomes, with context lengths reaching 131,072 base pairs. It covers conservation, splicing, regulatory signals, chromatin accessibility, and variant effects. Controlled experiments reportedly find that function-enriched sampling and redundancy filtering matter more than raw corpus size.

multiHIVE preserves shared and modality-specific biology

The hierarchical multimodal variational autoencoder represents processes shared across single-cell modalities while retaining modality-specific information in separate latent variables. The design avoids forcing meaningful, modality-unique variation into a single common space.

COSIGT genotypes difficult loci with pangenome graphs

COSIGT gathers aligned and rescued unmapped reads, maps them to a local pangenome graph, and compares observed node coverage with expected profiles for candidate diploid haplotype pairs. It targets complex loci that linear-reference methods can miss, including in low-coverage data.

Protein design and drug discovery

CAR-T results challenge standard computational binder filters

BAGEL-CAR submitted 414 AI-designed CD20-binding domains to a competition; 38.4% were enriched in a pooled CAR-T proliferation screen, reportedly the highest rate among 28 teams. ESMFold-based refolding and membrane-placement filters would have rejected every enriched design. One candidate later showed CD20-specific lysis and SPR binding near 643 nM, illustrating why structure confidence should not be treated as a universal proxy for cellular function.

CAR-TProtein designExperimental validation

OmniSyn generates molecules with explicit synthesis traces

OmniSyn combines protein-sequence conditioning, sparse mixture-of-experts routing, and a reaction-based decoder for de novo design, synthesizability projection, and hit-to-lead optimization. Outputs are assembled from more than 220,000 purchasable building blocks and 115 reaction templates. Its reported 2.7-billion-compound virtual library spans 21,306 human proteins, but target enrichment is supported mainly by computational checks rather than prospective assays.

Drug discoveryMolecular generationRetrosynthesis

NEAT-Pocket speeds up pocket-conditioned ligand generation

The model generates ligands atom by atom inside a protein pocket and reportedly samples 100 molecules in about four seconds on an RTX 4090. Its fine–coarse–fine encoder combines atomic and residue-level context, and it can grow molecules from fixed fragments while preserving their identity and pose.

3D generationBinding pocketsLead optimization

Inverse FoldDir tests flow-matched protein design in nanobodies

Inverse FoldDir performs structure-conditioned sequence design by iteratively denoising amino-acid probability distributions. In an anti-GFP experiment, two of 35 highly divergent nanobody designs showed reproducible binding. Boltz-2 confidence did not correlate with assay signal, reinforcing the limits of fold recovery and complex-confidence metrics as measures of function.

Français — Une grève des libraires, pour « faire souffler un vent nouveau sur la branche »

ActuaLitté · 7 septembre 2026

Les libraires de toute la France ont été appelés à faire grève le 8 septembre, à l’occasion de la reprise des négociations annuelles entre les syndicats de salariés et le Syndicat de la librairie française. Cette mobilisation, rare dans une profession dispersée entre de nombreuses petites entreprises indépendantes, vise principalement à obtenir de meilleurs salaires et à renforcer les droits prévus par la convention collective.

La CGT et SUD Culture Solidaires jugent notamment inacceptable que certains minima salariaux restent au niveau du Smic. Les organisations souhaitent aussi revoir le partage de la valeur dans l’ensemble de la chaîne du livre et réclament de nouveaux droits, tels qu’un treizième mois, des congés supplémentaires et une meilleure représentation collective dans les petites structures. Au-delà du rassemblement parisien, elles espèrent faire de cette journée le point de départ d’une organisation syndicale plus solide dans le secteur.

Pourquoi lire : L’article éclaire les réalités économiques et sociales souvent méconnues qui se cachent derrière le métier de libraire.

Vocabulaire utile

  • débrayer — cesser temporairement le travail pour participer à une grève
  • négociations annuelles obligatoires (NAO) — discussions périodiques imposées par le droit du travail sur les salaires et les conditions d’emploi
  • convention collective — accord fixant les droits et les conditions de travail propres à un secteur professionnel
  • grille de salaires — barème qui détermine les rémunérations minimales selon les postes et l’ancienneté

中文 — 毛泽东为何被一些中国“Z世代”追捧：弹幕、“教员”与政治失语

BBC 中文 · 2026-09-09

在中国年轻人的网络语境中，毛泽东常被称为“教员”。B站上由影视片段剪成的相关视频拥有数百万次观看，画面出现毛泽东时，弹幕中常见“全体起立”等赞颂性表达。《毛泽东选集》自2019年起也连续多年位居清华大学图书馆借阅榜榜首。

报道认为，这股热潮既受到官方“强国”叙事和历史教育的影响，也与青年面对的就业压力、贫富差距及阶层固化有关。一些年轻人借助毛泽东时代的政治语言，解释“内卷”、住房负担和发展机会不足等现实困境；但他们谈论历史人物时相对自在，涉及当代最高领导层则往往选择回避，反映出审查与自我审查之下的有限讨论空间。

为什么读： 文章从网络文化、青年处境和政治表达三个层面，分析毛泽东形象为何在部分中国年轻人中重新流行。

词汇笔记

  • 键政 (jiànzhèng) — 网民在网络平台上讨论、评论政治和时事的俗称。
