AI research

Goodfire maps 10 million Qwen3.6 neuron poles

Goodfire has released a searchable atlas of the positive and negative sides of neurons in Qwen3.6. Each “pole” gets a rough label from its maximally activating examples, with similarly labeled poles grouped nearby. A Library section examines 3,000 poles in depth, providing a more focused entry point into the interpretability resource.

Inside Marin Mochi’s architecture

Sebastian Raschka highlights design choices in Marin Mochi’s 535B-A23B checkpoint and technical report: extra RMSNorm in latent MoE, a 3:1 sliding-window/global gated-GQA mix, partial RoPE for local attention and NoPE for global attention, short convolutions on keys and attention/MoE outputs, and Exclusive Self-Attention. His analysis frames these choices around training stability and GPU throughput.

A challenge to the usual AdaGrad explanation

Francesco Orabona’s The Wrong Explanation of Preconditioning argues that the classic account of why preconditioning works in full-matrix AdaGrad does not make sense. In the discussion, Jianlin Su conjectures that spectral-norm-bound alignment is harder to achieve than behavior governed by the mean eigenvalue, explicitly presenting this as intuition without sufficient evidence.

Checking agent-written chip programs with heat

Yaroslav Bulatov describes asking agents to run computation on different regions of an AIFoundry open-source chip while watching the die through a thermal camera. The resulting heat patterns provide a physical sanity check on agent-directed programming.

Medical FAQ embeddings improve after domain fine-tuning

OpenMed reports NDCG@10 rising from 81.6 to 91.8 on 1,966 held-out medical FAQ questions after fine-tuning the 270M-parameter EmbeddingGemma 2 on MedQuAD. The Apache-2.0 model runs on CPU. A reported error ranks gout prevention above treatment for an acute attack; a GTE-ModernBERT sibling for literature search reports a smaller gain of 1.7.

Spanish clinical de-identification improves, with cross-language gaps

OpenMed introduces two Apache-2.0 models for masking identifiers in Spanish clinical reports, reporting character F2 rising from 35.8 to 98.6 on held-out reports and no alterations to 24 clean notes. Multilingual tests still missed an Italian tax code and fully masked only 61.7% of German full names. OpenMed positions the system as a screen for human review, not a guarantee.

Kishore Jaganathan announced that CellularAI won Arc Institute’s Virtual Cell Challenge for predicting perturbation responses in unseen cell lines. Arc reported 805 final team entries. Anshul Kundaje argued that the winning team had a data-access advantage, confounding methodological superiority with access to data. Does perturbation prediction teach causal regulation?

In a separate argument, Kundaje questions whether prioritizing trans-perturbation prediction moves the field away from learning causal gene regulation. His concern starts with experimental design: whether the collected data lets models learn the biology researchers actually want, rather than merely supporting a particular evaluation.

GENECraft designs regulatory DNA under explicit constraints

GENECraft combines frozen AlphaGenome predictions with a frozen Carbon sequence prior for training-free optimization. It evaluates discrete sequences throughout, enforces composition constraints and penalizes promoter-like activity when designing enhancers.

Across 2,185 human loci, the authors report computational full-joint success rates of 62.70% for promoters, 65.23% for enhancers and 94.56% for transcription-factor binding, using held-out Enformer and Borzoi predictions in evaluation. Prior guidance improves composition and robustness, but excessive weighting reduces yield.

DRAFT follows regulatory networks through cell trajectories

DRAFT combines graph attention with recurrent temporal memory to infer changing gene-regulatory networks along single-cell trajectories. Single-cell & genomics

scUNVEIL learns cell embeddings by predicting the next UMI

scUNVEIL learns measurement-enriched transcriptome representations directly from count vectors using a residual multilayer perceptron, rather than attention over gene tokens. Its low-dimensional hidden state becomes the cell embedding. The framework connects its Bayes-optimal next-UMI prediction target to the posterior mean transcriptome composition that deeper sequencing would reveal.

scUNVEIL research summary

CIRCA separates cellular and neighborhood programs

CIRCA applies grouped causal representation learning to spatial transcriptomics, jointly learning cell-intrinsic and neighborhood-level transcriptional programs and sparse directed interactions between them. Its identifiability claims depend on explicit assumptions about the data-generating process.

CIRCA research summary

SPiCe models cellular plasticity across tissue environments

SPiCe combines graph neural networks and geostatistics to study intrinsic genomic and extrinsic microenvironmental contributions to cellular plasticity. Graph models capture nonlinear higher-order interactions; spatial error models and geographically weighted regression quantify heterogeneity and short- versus long-range influences.

SPiCe research summary

scHiCGNN treats chromatin contacts as graphs

scHiCGNN encodes each chromosome’s Hi-C contact matrix as a weighted graph, learns node features through multi-head graph attention, and combines chromosome embeddings with lightweight self-attention. This reframes single-cell Hi-C clustering as graph representation learning rather than flattened-vector PCA.

scHiCGNN research summary

HARICOT assembles directly from raw nanopore signals

HARICOT uses fuzzy de Bruijn graphs and Hough-transform-based overlap detection to assemble noisy raw nanopore signals. Reported results improve contiguity and assembly quality over Rawsamble, narrowing the gap to nucleotide-sequence assemblers such as Flye and wtdbg2.

HARICOT research summary

Repeated data and layer looping cut pretraining data needs

Q Labs combines repeated-data training, shared-weight layer looping and snapshot ensembling across molecular, DNA and crystal tasks. The study reports matching baseline performance with up to 56× less unique molecular data and 24× less DNA or crystal data, with some gains extrapolated.

Downstream results reportedly exceed NovoMolGen on eight MoleculeNet tasks using approximately 350× less pretraining data and match HybriDNA on GUE using approximately 800× less.

Pretraining study

TEN targets feature selection when samples are scarce

TEN pairs gene-expression-oriented data augmentation with a transformer encoder for high-dimensional, low-sample feature selection. It uses biological characteristics of expression data to enhance class-discriminative differences, with self-attention modeling relationships among latent sample representations.

Molecular design & medicine

GRABS exposes a consequential affinity-data misalignment

GRABS curates 4,380 antibody–antigen affinity records, with predicted complexes and features for 4,282. Sequence-derived content keys connect measurements to artifacts to prevent silent positional misalignment. Correcting a misaligned feature store changed pooled cross-target R² from approximately 0.415 to −0.2, the authors report.

Under antigen-cluster-held-out evaluation, within-antigen variant ranking falls to approximately zero across sequence, structural and confidence-based models, motivating workflows that measure examples on a target before ranking variants.

GRABS research summary

Affinity transfer has target-specific limits

A companion study proposes a noise-corrected, censoring-aware transfer ceiling separating transferable antibody effects from target-specific interactions. Tested methods show near-zero held-out-antigen variant ranking on GRABS, while a structural GNN reaches Spearman 0.548 for within-complex ranking on SKEMPI 2.0.

Removing antigen nodes barely changes that model’s performance, suggesting much of its signal is antibody-intrinsic. Retrospective acquisition simulations favor target-specific measurement before model-guided ranking over zero-shot cross-target transfer.

Affinity-transfer study

STORM offers a lightweight, structure-free affinity baseline

STORM ensembles 15 small neural networks using molecular fingerprints and protein annotations, without complex structures, MSAs or large foundation-model embeddings. The authors report within-assay Pearson correlation of approximately 0.355 on a ChEMBL35 time split versus 0.303 for Boltz-2 in a matched evaluation, with broadly comparable results on three other benchmarks.

Inference takes approximately 17.9 microseconds per prediction on an NVIDIA L4. Target-specific fine-tuning improves performance on all three tested targets absent from training.

STORM research summary

TMol moves differentiable Rosetta-style energetics to GPUs

TMol implements RosettaGenFF scoring and refinement as GPU-native PyTorch operations, with automatic differentiation and automated parameterization for ligands, modified residues and nucleotides. Reported speedups over single-threaded PyRosetta reach 233–362× for scoring plus gradients and 38–102× for torsion-space relaxation; total energies correlate at 0.997 across 1,000 protein chains.

A design demonstration improves interface hydrogen bonding, although forward-folding success is comparable to best-of-k selection. The software uses the BSD-3 license.

TMol research summary

Generated alignments show signals—and generalization failures

Sergey Ovchinnikov reports that Claude Fable 5.2 can generate multiple-sequence alignments with coevolutionary signals usable by AlphaFold. Follow-up tests find poor contact accuracy for orphan proteins without UniProt homologs and unsuccessful paired-MSA generation for protein–protein interactions.

Ovchinnikov hypothesizes that this pattern could reflect training on individual UniProt sequences rather than genomic context.

Experiments and discussion

MARRI adds accessibility and pairing constraints to RNA predictions

MARRI combines RNA foundation-model embeddings, structural features and biological metadata with cross-RNA attention incorporating opening penalties and hybridization gains. Reported ROC–AUC is 74.27% on a shared test set, falling to 66.30% when entire genes are held out.

Predicted nucleotide-pair hotspots respond to computational perturbations and partially agree with resolved contacts in three complexes. Disease-expression analyses use the predictions to build candidate interaction networks in diabetic nephropathy and atherosclerosis.

MARRI research summary

Deutsch — In Dutzenden Städten: Jugendproteste in ganz Deutschland

Tagesschau · 20.11.2026 · Artikel lesen

Für heute haben mehrere Jugendorganisationen zu Demonstrationen in Dutzenden deutschen Städten aufgerufen. Beteiligt sind unter anderem „Gen Z regelt“, die Bundesschülerkonferenz, Fridays For Future und „Schulstreik gegen die Wehrpflicht“. Ihre Anliegen überschneiden sich, sind aber nicht identisch: Es geht um bessere Bildung, steigende Lebenshaltungskosten, Klimaschutz und die Frage, wie gesellschaftliche Lasten zwischen den Generationen verteilt werden. Hinzu kommen Proteste gegen Überwachung sowie gegen Wehrpflicht und Musterung.

Der Beitrag stellt diesen Forderungen die Reaktionen der Bundesregierung gegenüber. Bildungsministerin Karin Prien verspricht, junge Menschen stärker an politischen Entscheidungen zu beteiligen, und betont die Bedeutung von Bildung für Generationengerechtigkeit. Zugleich erinnert der Artikel an die Proteste vom 31. Oktober: Damals demonstrierten Zehntausende überwiegend friedlich; später kam es in einigen Städten vereinzelt zu Ausschreitungen. Bundeskanzler Friedrich Merz hatte die Anliegen der Jugendlichen anschließend als legitim anerkannt.

Warum lesen: Der Artikel bietet einen kompakten Einstieg in die unterschiedlichen Forderungen der Jugendproteste und die politische Debatte über Generationengerechtigkeit in Deutschland.

Français — Sommet de l'Apec : Xi Jinping reçoit Vladimir Poutine avant un sommet inédit avec Donald Trump

France 24 · 18 novembre 2026 · Lire l’article

Dans cet article publié avant la rencontre annoncée pour le 19 novembre, France 24 présente les enjeux du rapprochement diplomatique entre Xi Jinping, Vladimir Poutine et Donald Trump à Shenzhen, en marge de l’Apec. L’Ukraine, l’Europe et Taïwan redoutent des décisions prises sans leur participation. La comparaison avec la conférence de Yalta illustre cette crainte d’un partage des zones d’influence entre grandes puissances, même si les experts cités soulignent que le sommet pourrait aussi déboucher sur peu de résultats concrets.

Le principal point d’incertitude concerne l’ordre du jour : Pékin évoque la paix et la stabilité stratégique, tandis que Donald Trump mentionne la dénucléarisation, la guerre en Iran et l’Ukraine. Le texte distingue également les objectifs des trois dirigeants : consolider la place internationale de la Chine, affirmer que la Russie reste une grande puissance malgré les sanctions et obtenir, pour le président américain, des gains diplomatiques après un revers électoral. Il s’agit donc d’une lecture des attentes et des rapports de force avant le sommet, et non d’un bilan de ses conclusions.

Pourquoi lire : Pour comprendre les inquiétudes européennes face à une diplomatie entre grandes puissances et découvrir le vocabulaire des négociations internationales.

Vocabulaire utile

  • sommet trilatéral — rencontre de haut niveau réunissant les représentants de trois États.
  • stabilité stratégique — équilibre entre puissances visant à limiter les risques de confrontation majeure, notamment nucléaire.
  • dénucléarisation — processus de suppression ou de réduction des armes nucléaires dans un pays ou une région.

日本語 — H3ロケット 次の11号機の打ち上げ業務を民営化へ 三菱重工業に

NHKニュース · 2026年11月20日 · 記事を読む

日本の主力ロケットH3の打ち上げ業務が、次の11号機からJAXAから三菱重工業へ移管される見通しだ。NHKによると、今年度に入って3機連続で打ち上げに成功し、運用が安定してきたことを受け、両者が民営化で合意した。まず対象となるのは補助ロケットを2本備えるタイプで、ほかのタイプも段階的な民営化を検討する。

狙いは、海外の衛星事業者からも注文を獲得し、衛星打ち上げを本格的な事業として広げることにある。専門家は、長期契約が機体の効率的な生産につながり、国際競争力を高める可能性を指摘する。一方、先代のH2Aは海外勢との競争で海外衛星の打ち上げ受注が5件にとどまった。H3が技術面の成功を商業的な実績につなげられるかが、今後の課題となる。

読む理由： 日本の宇宙開発が、技術を確立する段階から国際市場で事業を広げる段階へどう移るのかを、具体的に読める。

語彙メモ

  • 移管（いかん） — 業務の管理や責任を別の組織へ移すこと。
  • 民営化（みんえいか） — 公的な組織が担っていた事業や業務を民間企業が担う形に変えること。
  • 受注（じゅちゅう） — 商品の製造やサービスの提供について、顧客から注文を受けること。

中文 — APEC的特色服饰：从克林顿皮衣到深圳“鲲鹏展翅”丝巾

BBC 中文 · 2026-11-19 · https://www.bbc.com/zhongwen/articles/cmly7er4rze9o/simp

峰会合照里的服饰，也能成为观察外交与文化展示的窗口。深圳APEC峰会为领导人准备了以“鲲鹏展翅”为主题的红蓝丝绸围巾。报道借此回顾这项惯例的起源：1993年，克林顿向与会领导人赠送皮衣，随后其他主办国陆续用本国特色服饰迎接来宾，让严肃的国际会议多了一点轻松气氛。

中国此前主办峰会时推出的“新唐装”和“新中装”，把传统服装元素与现代设计结合起来，立领、对襟、盘纽等细节成为展示文化身份的方式。不过，统一着装也受到流于表演的批评，有些主办国选择让领导人穿西装合影。近年，秘鲁、韩国和此次深圳都采用围巾，显示这项惯例正从整套服装转向更简洁的配饰。

为什么读： 从一张峰会合照切入，既能了解文化展示与外交礼仪的关系，也能积累描述中式服装的专门词汇。

词汇笔记

  • 对襟 (duì jīn) — 上衣前面左右两片衣襟在正中相对、扣合的式样。
  • 盘纽 (pán niǔ) — 用布条或绳带盘结而成的纽扣，兼具扣合与装饰作用。
  • 蜡染 (là rǎn) — 用蜡遮盖布料的部分区域后染色，再去蜡形成花纹的工艺。
