AI Research Tools & Multimodal Methods

Silico automates replication and extension of AI research experiments

Goodfire presents Silico, an experimental platform for automating research reproduction and follow-up work. Reported demonstrations include replicating J-space on GLM-5.2 and extending multi-hop question answering to contexts of roughly 256,000 tokens.

The team also reports reproducing RLFR in two days, reducing Qwen3-8B hallucinations by 37% without capability loss, finding protein-structure-correlated subspaces with Block-Sparse Featurizers, and replicating PICASSO for pathology-model interpretation.

Research automation · Interpretability · Reinforcement learning · Long context

NicheTrans translates spatial biological data across modalities

Nature Methods highlights a multimodal framework for translating spatial biological data between modalities. The supplied material does not include methodological or benchmark details, but the approach may be useful for integrating spatial-omics measurements collected through different technologies.

Structural transfer appears stronger from language to biology than in reverse

A controlled study reports asymmetric transfer between natural-language and protein models. Language-task training transfers above chance to protein homology, while protein training and continued pretraining provide little reliable benefit on language-structure tasks. Architecture-matched comparisons and shuffled-protein controls suggest differences in learnability rather than a universally shared representation space.

Protein Design, Structure & Therapeutics

Designed proteins selectively inhibit or activate kinases

A preprint uses RFdiffusion, ProteinMPNN, Rosetta and AlphaFold2 to design compact proteins against distinct conformations of focal adhesion kinase. Screening produced 30 inhibitors and three activators, including nanomolar modulators that changed FAK signaling, adhesion phenotypes and cancer-spheroid viability. Successful scaffolds were also retargeted to Src.

The work suggests that conformational ensembles can serve as programmable targets for bidirectional control of cell signaling. Delivery, immunogenicity and in-vivo validation remain unresolved.

Protein design · Kinases · Cancer biology · Drug discovery

GPFlow enables variable-length protein and peptide generation

GPFlow combines an insertion process that changes sequence length with within-length generative dynamics, removing the need to specify length before sampling. Reported applications include unconditional sequence and structure generation, motif scaffolding and full-atom peptide co-design, with improvements in designability or diversity over fixed-length baselines in several evaluations.

Protein design · Peptide design · Generative models · Flow matching

AlphaFold 3 assessment finds persistent specificity gaps

A broad evaluation covers ubiquitination, TCR–epitope recognition, antibody–antigen complexes, protein–RNA interactions and protein–lipid co-folding. AlphaFold 3 often localizes interfaces and can prioritize some candidates, but struggles with partner specificity, mutation effects, affinity calibration and choosing the right model across seeds. Reduced training-set overlap further lowers performance.

AlphaFold 3 · Model evaluation · Immunology · Molecular interactions

RNAbpFlow generates all-atom RNA conformational ensembles

RNAbpFlow is designed to generate all-atom conformational ensembles for single-chain RNA monomers without evolutionary information or homologous structures. Detailed benchmarks were not available in the supplied report, but the method could be relevant to RNA structural biology and therapeutic discovery.

RNA · Structural biology · Conformational ensembles · Generative models

CPP2Vec offers lightweight peptide-delivery prediction

CPP2Vec combines Skip-Gram peptide embeddings with conventional classifiers to predict cell penetration, uptake efficiency and phosphorodiamidate morpholino oligomer delivery. It reports performance comparable to heavier protein language models at much lower CPU cost. A small Duchenne muscular dystrophy study links predicted delivery scores with dystrophin-restoration measurements.

Drug delivery · Peptides · Antisense · Machine learning

Active learning optimizes concentrated antibody formulations

An automated Design–Build–Test–Learn platform combines robotic formulation, high-throughput assays and Gaussian-process Bayesian optimization. Starting with 24 seed formulations, two active-learning rounds searched a space of about 800 million combinations and improved melting temperature, diffusivity and viscosity after only 72 experiments. SHAP analysis identified arginine as a major source of trade-offs.

Active learning · Lab automation · Antibodies · Biologics development

A geometric atlas traces modality fusion inside ESM3

An interpretability study follows sequence, structure tokens, secondary structure, solvent accessibility and functional annotations through ESM3’s 48 layers. Physical modalities undergo a sharp fusion transition around layers 25–35, with sequence joining structure-derived information relatively late; functional annotations remain largely orthogonal. Controls suggest the geometry is learned rather than an architectural artifact.

Mechanistic interpretability · ESM3 · Multimodal protein models

Genomics, Cells & Biological Foundation Models

The BioCollection assembles a 52.6-billion-token biology corpus

THEBIOCOLLECTION unifies training-ready records covering small molecules, proteins, genomic sequences, cells and pathways, with computed properties and instruction tasks. Its evaluation suite spans recognition, prediction, generation and cross-domain reasoning. Continued training of a fixed 16B architecture reportedly more than doubled aggregate biological performance while preserving general language ability.

Datasets · Biological language models · Genomics · Benchmarks

ARSENAL learns regulatory DNA from short enriched sequences

ARSENAL is a masked DNA language model trained on about 2.3 million ENCODE candidate cis-regulatory elements using 350-base-pair contexts. Its zero-shot likelihood maps recover motifs and dependencies, improve regulatory-QTL scoring, and support ChromBPNet count and variant-effect prediction. It can also guide in-silico design of cell-type-specific accessible sequences, though experimental validation is still needed.

Regulatory genomics · DNA language models · Variant effects · Sequence design

Cherimoya targets efficient local regulatory-function prediction

Cherimoya is presented as a smaller, faster sequence-to-function model for predicting local regulatory activity. The accompanying report emphasizes extensive hyperparameter exploration and practical efficiency improvements, with a code repository available for further study.

Genomics · Gene regulation · Sequence-to-function · Efficient models

Single-cell foundation models show task-dependent utility

A systematic analysis compares 20 pretrained single-cell representation methods across six downstream tasks and 1,607 datasets containing nearly 21.8 million cells. No method consistently dominates, and strong performance on original datasets often fails to remain robust when cell counts, gene counts, class balance, composition or batch complexity change.

Single-cell · Foundation models · Robustness · Model evaluation

FIERCE reconstructs cellular trajectories with signaling entropy

FIERCE reconstructs dynamics from single-cell RNA sequencing by estimating each cell’s future signaling entropy, calculating entropy velocity and generating a vector field over a differentiation-potency landscape. The resulting streamplots aim to show paths cells follow during differentiation.

Single-cell · Cell trajectories · Differentiation · Systems biology

Tahoe-100M is presented as a large single-cell perturbation atlas for studying context-dependent gene function and cellular modeling. The supplied report contained only the resource title and a shortened link, so dataset composition and validation results remain unavailable.

Français — Fin de vie : l’aide à mourir, un « long cheminement » pour Emmanuel Macron

Le Monde · 15 juillet 2026 · https://www.lemonde.fr/politique/article/2026/07/15/fin-de-vie-l-aide-a-mourir-un-long-cheminement-pour-emmanuel-macron_6723495_823448.html

L’article retrace l’évolution prudente d’Emmanuel Macron sur l’aide à mourir. Le chef de l’État a longtemps douté de la nécessité d’une nouvelle législation, craignant notamment de heurter les représentants des cultes et d’accentuer les divisions de la société française sur une question éthique particulièrement sensible.

Le récit souligne aussi l’importance politique du choix des mots : Emmanuel Macron ne souhaitait employer ni « euthanasie » ni « suicide », et l’académicien Erik Orsenna aurait été sollicité pour chercher une formulation différente. Cette maturation aboutit à une proposition de loi sur l’aide à mourir, soumise au vote final de l’Assemblée nationale le 15 juillet.

Pourquoi lire : Pour comprendre comment une réforme majeure de la fin de vie s’est construite à la fois par des arbitrages politiques, éthiques et lexicaux.

Vocabulaire utile

  • aide à mourir — dispositif encadré permettant, sous certaines conditions légales et médicales, d’accompagner une personne qui demande à mettre fin à sa vie
  • fin de vie — période où une personne souffre d’une maladie grave, incurable ou arrivée à un stade avancé
  • représentants des cultes — responsables qui parlent au nom des différentes institutions religieuses
  • fracturer la société — provoquer une division profonde et durable au sein de la population

中文 — AI热潮推高中国进出口 对德国贸易顺差明显

德国之声 · 2026年7月14日 · https://www.dw.com/zh/ai热潮推高中国进出口-对德国贸易顺差明显/a-77950854

中国6月对德国出口额同比增长27.2%，从德国进口额却只增长3.1%。今年上半年，中国对德商品出口总额为675亿美元，进口额为452亿美元，使德国对华贸易赤字达到223亿美元。报道指出，过去以汽车、机械和特种技术出口见长的德国，如今正面对中国电动汽车及机械设备带来的更强竞争，同时在电池和医药前体产品等领域仍依赖中国供应。

人工智能热潮也显著推高了中国整体进出口金额，其中半导体出口额同比翻倍，数据处理设备出口额增长53.1%。不过，分析认为增长主要来自存储芯片短缺造成的价格上涨，而非实际出口数量增加。随着中国汽车出口激增、稀土出口管制收紧，欧洲与中国之间的产业竞争和贸易摩擦可能继续升温。
