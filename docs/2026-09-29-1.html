AI & computation

Training efficiency

NanoGPT speedrun: 39.9 seconds with massive sparse embeddings

Larry Dial reports a run on 8×H100 that cuts training time from 67.6 to 39.9 seconds. The implementation combines sampled softmax, sparse embedding updates and communication, compressed optimizer states, custom attention for 64-dimensional heads, late-stage exponential moving averages, and Anvil2, an optimizer extending Muon.

The unusual trade-off: active parameters stay below 124 million while sparse embeddings grow to 65 billion. You Jiacheng cautions that sampled softmax’s scalability remains unresolved and embeddings at this scale would require new infrastructure. This is a reported speedrun result, not an established general-purpose scaling recipe.

A theoretical route from Kronecker preconditioning to Muon

Jianlin Su’s “Orthogonal Slowest Descent” note starts from a convex-optimization convergence bound. The proposed construction removes a Kronecker preconditioner’s eigenvectors and retains its eigenvalues to balance the gradient spectrum, leading to Muon.

Biological design & drug discovery

Experimental RNA design

DS3dRNA’s eight tested designs show functional activity

DS3dRNA uses three-body spatial interactions to score sequences for RNA scaffolds, including multiple conformations. All three tested Mango II aptamer designs activated fluorescence, and all five tested twister ribozymes cleaved substrate.

Under the reported conditions, the ribozymes reached 37.7–50.6% cleavage at 20 seconds, versus 23.5% for wild type. The functional results are encouraging but cover just eight designs; computational structural comparisons remain predictions, not measured structures.

DS3dRNA research summary

AnewDDE reports 16 nanomolar nanobody binders

A closed-loop system links structure prediction, affinity estimation, molecular design, reasoning, and experimental feedback. Across two design–test–learn rounds and 150 tested clones, the authors report 16 nanobodies with single-digit nanomolar affinity measured by surface plasmon resonance.

The experimental campaign involved one internal protein target. Broader generalization and the fully integrated workflow still require validation.

AnewDDE research summary

Targeted Boltz-1 tuning recovers difficult WRN states

Using ten WRN structures and updating roughly 1% of Boltz-1’s parameters, researchers report recovering an inhibitor-induced allosteric pocket and an approximately 180° domain rotation missed by several zero-shot cofolding models. Improvements extended to 75 unseen WRN–ligand complexes.

An ATP-bound training example was needed to avoid indiscriminately predicting the closed state. The result concerns structural end states—not transition dynamics, potency, or selectivity.

WRN fine-tuning summary

TCR prediction: larger representations do not solve weak labels

For two peptide–MHC targets, ESM-2 and ProtGPT2 representations generally performed no better than one-hot or BLOSUM62 encodings. Results depended strongly on the epitope and how presumed non-binders were constructed.

Neither shuffled antigen associations nor healthy-donor repertoires establish experimental non-binding. The study argues for validated negatives and clearer evaluation reporting, though its two-epitope scope limits generalization.

TCR specificity study summary

DELPHI’s antibody predictions face an external-transfer test

DELPHI combines protein-language-model benchmarking, CDR H3-cluster cross-validation, learning curves, and residue attribution. Reported internal mean AUCs are 0.959 for polyreactivity and 0.933 for SEC outcomes; a blinded external assay shows more modest transfer, with absolute Spearman correlation of 0.35.

Positive charge and longer CDR H3 regions are associated with failure risk in attribution analyses. These are predictive associations, not established causal engineering rules.

DELPHI research summary

PepSpace searches for selective peptides—in simulation

PepSpace combines evolutionary search with coarse-grained molecular dynamics, rewarding target engagement while penalizing self-association and unwanted contacts. CHERP-targeting designs weakened simulated protein interactions and lowered simulated condensation critical temperature by up to 13.3 K; another example targeted a small PIEZO1 epitope.

All reported demonstrations are computational. Binding, selectivity, cellular activity, and delivery still need experimental tests.

PepSpace research summary

LoGoPPI scales interaction screening, with a hard-negative warning

Reusable global protein embeddings and local residue matching reportedly enabled an all-pairs screen of 44,776 quinoa proteins in under a day. Hard-negative evaluations reduced performance to approximately 0.64 AUPR, underscoring how negative selection changes the apparent quality of a predictor.

The interaction network is predicted rather than experimentally validated, and sequences longer than 800 residues are truncated.

Cells, RNA & regulatory genetics

Pop-Corn predicts who is in the cell population

Rather than reconstructing expression, Pop-Corn directly predicts changes in cell-type and cell-state proportions after genetic perturbations. The supplied description reports tests on unseen T-cell perturbations and extension to local cellular composition in tissues.

The approach targets diversity and rare-cell shifts that average-expression metrics can miss. Numerical results were not supplied.

RNASeek brings sequence, structure, and language into one vocabulary

RNASeek is described as a 1.6-billion-parameter model using a DeepSeek architecture and trained on a cross-phyla transcriptomic corpus. Its vocabulary combines nucleotides, genomic annotations, secondary structures, and approximately 12,000 natural-language tokens.

Posts report zero-shot capture of transcript features and intron–exon boundaries, conditional generation, and reinforcement-learning-based design. Quantitative benchmarks and experimental validation are not included.

Français — 200 ans qu'on cherchait cette traduction : elle était dans la bibliothèque de Goethe

ActuaLitté · 26 septembre 2026 · https://actualitte.com/article/134210/bibliophilie/200-ans-qu-on-cherchait-cette-traduction-elle-etait-dans-la-bibliotheque-de-goethe

À Weimar, une traduction italienne d’Iphigénie en Tauride, longtemps considérée comme perdue, a été identifiée dans la bibliothèque personnelle de Goethe. Le manuscrit était pourtant conservé et répertorié : ce qui manquait, c’était le nom de son traducteur. L’historienne de l’art Francesca Fabbri l’a attribué au poète napolitain Alessandro Poerio, qui avait séjourné à Weimar en 1825 et 1826 et offert son travail à l’écrivain allemand.

Pour résoudre cette énigme, la chercheuse a repris une étude publiée en 1882 par le bibliothécaire Reinhold Köhler et l’a confrontée au document anonyme. L’article montre ainsi la différence entre localiser un objet dans une collection et comprendre son histoire. Cette attribution éclaire aussi les échanges littéraires entre l’Allemagne et l’Italie, à travers un traducteur qui fut également engagé dans le mouvement d’unification italien.

Fabbri a parallèlement retrouvé à Chicago le manuscrit autographe d’une autre traduction de Poerio, La sposa di Corinto, également remise à Goethe. Les deux documents peuvent désormais être étudiés ensemble. L’Iphigénie italienne doit être présentée à la Casa di Goethe de Rome dans une exposition annoncée de décembre 2026 à avril 2027 ; une édition scientifique est prévue pour l’automne 2027.

Pourquoi lire : Une enquête sur les manuscrits qui relie patrimoine allemand, traduction italienne et travail concret des chercheurs en bibliothèque.

Vocabulaire utile

  • cote — code attribué à un document pour l’identifier et le retrouver dans une bibliothèque.
  • manuscrit autographe — texte écrit de la main même de son auteur ou, ici, de son traducteur.
  • attribution — identification de la personne à laquelle on doit une œuvre ou un document, à partir d’indices et de recherches.

日本語 — 怪異撮影ホラーゲーム『新宿異変』9月30日配信へ。怪異を撮影する“だけ”のアルバイト、アップで撮りたいけど、近寄り過ぎてはダメ

AUTOMATON · 2026-09-28 · https://automaton-media.com/articles/newsjp/20260928-470342/

AMATA Gamesは、夜の新宿で怪異を撮影するホラービジュアルノベル『新宿異変』を、9月30日10時にPC向けのSteamで配信すると発表した。価格は税込500円。短編ながら複数の結末が用意されており、実際の新宿で撮影した風景を加工したとみられる背景が、身近な街に潜む恐怖を演出する。

プレイヤーの仕事は、怪異の写真を撮るアルバイトだ。人影を見つけたら、近づくか、撮影するか、その場を離れるかを選ぶ。撮影には適切な距離まで接近する必要がある一方、近づきすぎると危険にさらされる。普通の人間を誤って撮影することにも注意が必要で、こうした判断が物語の行方を左右する。

記事では、作品が『８番出口』『孵道』や伊藤潤二氏の漫画から影響を受けたことも紹介している。『孵道』とは別の作品だが、発表当時にデザインの類似が話題となり、開発者間のやりとりを経て一部を変更したという制作上の経緯にも触れている。

読む理由： 新宿という実在の街を舞台に、単純な選択と距離感で恐怖を生むゲームの仕組みを、日本語の紹介記事で具体的に読める。

中文 — 有自由度的平台跳跃：《噬莱姆计划》9月28日发售

机核 · 2026-09-28 · https://www.gcores.com/articles/220329

据机核介绍，奇怪波浪工作室研发、Another Indie 发行的《噬莱姆计划》于9月28日在 Steam 发售。这款解谜平台跳跃游戏把场景中的方块变成可以操控的资源：玩家不只是寻找落脚点，还能吞下、吐出方块，重新安排前进路线。
