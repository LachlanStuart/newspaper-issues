AI research & training

Model weights as hardware fingerprints

Will Depue reports recovering hardware-dependent initialization patterns from released model weights after searching more than six trillion GPU-layout and random-seed combinations. He attributes six models’ initialization to Blackwell systems, including bit-for-bit reproduction of untouched DeepSeek-V4-Flash weights on B300 and a 0.999995 correlation for Kimi K3 on GB200. He also reports validation against 24 models with known hardware.

In replies, Lawrence Chan asks how preserved rows were selected, how many independently seeded processes support the matches, and how estimates of training-token counts account for learning rate, batch size and schedule.

Selective teaching for document learning

Stanford AI Lab highlights Speculative Self-Distillation, a NeurIPS 2026 paper aimed at teaching an LLM a new document without forgetting existing knowledge. The student generates text while a teacher intervenes selectively; related discussion emphasizes accuracy, efficiency, forgetting and the problem of early student mistakes during on-policy rollouts.

Two challenges for reasoning monitors

Sebastian Raschka discusses research distinguishing reasoning without written chain of thought from the ability to alter written reasoning on request. He reports that better pretraining increases the former, while reinforcement learning substantially increases the latter—and that controllability can transfer through distillation.

Both complicate monitoring, which depends on written reasoning reflecting actual computation. Architecture mostly did not matter in the results he discusses, and process supervision was not the main culprit.

A CPU-ready biomedical extraction fine-tune

OpenMed reports that fine-tuning GLiNER2.5 Small on 800 MedMentions abstracts raised F1 from 45.1 to 67.4 in a single evaluation on 200 held-out abstracts. Incorrect tags fell from 1,399 to 678.

The 74-million-parameter, Apache-2.0 model runs on CPU. Its extraction scope covers drugs, not doses, and treats enzymes and receptors as chemicals.

OpenCLIP adds layer-wise learning-rate decay

Ross Wightman has added layer-wise learning-rate decay for both text and image encoders, which he says could reduce forgetting when adapting under large distribution shifts. He cautions that further contrastive fine-tuning of CLIP often struggles with insufficient or low-quality data, especially far from the pretraining distribution.

Proteins & molecular design

ProTEUS closes the loop on experimental protein evolution

ProTEUS combines sequence, evolutionary and structural representations with mutation-biased attention, pretraining on 182 deep-mutational-scanning assays. The authors report improved held-out fitness ranking and extrapolation to higher-order mutants; increasing activity-training data approximately 80-fold reduced test MSE from 0.262 to 0.115.

Closed-loop campaigns testing 100–150 sequences each produced levansucrase variants with up to 14.03-fold wild-type activity and ALDH variants with approximately 6.85-fold total activity and 7.90-fold normalized catalytic rate.

Cadense samples antibody conformations

Cadense is an MSA-free diffusion model generating antibody Fv ensembles in seconds to under a minute, including apo-like and holo-like CDR-H3 conformations. Its distribution-focused variant reports a median flexibility correlation with molecular dynamics of 0.84 across 22 held-out antibodies, versus 0.57 for Boltz-2.

Ensemble-based surface analysis reduced false high-risk classifications 3.5-fold relative to a static first-frame baseline. Ensemble inputs also improved HADDOCK3 docking on 44 internal antibody–antigen complexes.

Cadense research summary

OriDynamics models proteins with their partners

OriDynamics combines latent-state generation with all-atom diffusion refinement, training against ensemble statistics such as atomic fluctuations and coordinate covariance. The authors report improved ligand and pocket flexibility on MISATO, better recovery of alternative experimental conformations, and partner-dependent shifts between apo and holo ensembles.

Transient interchain contact rearrangements remain difficult. Adaptation to ligand-dissociation data improves coverage of displaced and unbinding-like configurations.

OriDynamics research summary

291 million spectra underpin π-SPECFormer

π-SPECFormer combines self-supervised spectral pretraining with peptide–spectrum-match-guided refinement, reporting improvements in de novo peptide sequencing, spectrum clustering and phosphorylation detection.

Its workflow identified 5,103 candidate amino-acid substitutions across 1,712 proteins in samples from 103 lung adenocarcinoma patients. Synthetic peptide spectra supported nine high-confidence mutated peptides. Cell-line experiments suggested that PYGL p.A303S counteracts wild-type PYGL’s proliferation-promoting effect; predicted mutation–drug-target interactions remain testable hypotheses.

π-SPECFormer research summary

Better protein probes do not guarantee sequence control

A study uses generative latent priors on frozen ESM-2 activations to interpret and steer protein representations. Individual latent units outperform single sparse-autoencoder features on discrete biological-concept probes, but combinations of SAE features usually match them—suggesting concentration of existing information rather than discovery of new information.

Activation correction improves biochemical-property steering, but the benefit disappears when generated sequences are re-embedded. Structural steering consistently succeeds only for helices; long-range motifs remain problematic.

Protein interpretability study

Epistasis adds predictive power—and complexity

An interpretable sequence-to-phenotype framework learns relevant alignment positions, interacting residue pairs and position-specific amino-acid groupings from roughly 100 sequence–phenotype pairs. Across 13 case studies, its combined model is competitive with phylogenetic imputation and ESM-2 regression, particularly for temperature-related traits and HIV protease drug resistance.

Adding epistasis improves prediction but expands selected positions from about 5% to about 30% of the alignment, trading simplicity for distributed interaction signals.

Epistasis framework

SensDiff trains with chemical constraints

SensDiff progressively introduces steric, bond-length, bond-angle and valence constraints during diffusion training, applying them to intermediate denoising states rather than only checking final molecules.

The authors report better molecular validity and stability on QM9 and GEOM-Drugs, alongside lower errors in quantum-property-conditioned generation. A constrained-design demonstration steers sampling toward up to seven drug-like property objectives without retraining.

AlphaGenome 2 tops an updated enhancer benchmark

Al Murphy reports that AlphaGenome 2 leads an updated enhancer benchmark, describing its gains as clear but incremental and highlighting better performance on enhancers out to roughly 100 kb. Related commentary from antisense proposes testing how prediction confidence changes with cell number and data quality in unseen states, followed by experimental validation in the relevant cells.

RawMod detects modifications from nanopore signals

RawMod is a transformer-based, control-free method for detecting epigenetically modified genomic positions. It compares raw signals from multiple reads at each site with the unmodified reference sequence, learning different modification types together as one modified class rather than requiring a separate classifier for each chemistry.

RawMod

SpaAxFormer links genes across neighboring cells

SpaAxFormer uses axial transformers to contextualize genes within each cell, then exchanges corresponding-gene representations across neighboring cells. A separate cell-level summary is trained through expression reconstruction and contrastive learning, connecting gene-level modeling with spatial cellular representations.

SpaAxFormer

scBoost stacks single-cell embeddings

scBoost-ensemble trains an XGBoost classifier on each embedding view in an AnnData object, then combines out-of-fold class probabilities through a meta-learner. It brings together representations from methods such as Harmony, Scanorama, scVI, scGPT and scANVI to improve cell-type classification.

Deutsch — Die AfD und das Neonazi-Milieu: Wie eng sind die Verbindungen?

Deutsche Welle · 13. November 2026 · Artikel lesen

Wie glaubwürdig grenzt sich die AfD von neonazistischen Organisationen ab? Die DW untersucht personelle Verbindungen und lässt den Rechtsextremismusforscher Fabian Virchow zu Wort kommen. Er beschreibt eine Normalisierung rechtsextremer Positionen innerhalb der Partei, die dadurch für das neonazistische Milieu attraktiver werde. Der Beitrag ordnet diese Entwicklung auch anhand der Leipziger Autoritarismus-Studie ein: Ihr zufolge vereint die AfD große Teile der Wählerschaft mit antidemokratischen Einstellungen auf sich.

Im Mittelpunkt stehen Fälle aus Sachsen-Anhalt. Zwei AfD-Landtagsabgeordnete waren laut Bericht von einer Razzia wegen des Verdachts betroffen, die verbotene „Artgemeinschaft“ fortzuführen; ihre Anwälte bestreiten die Vorwürfe. Zudem verweist die DW auf Medienrecherchen über ehemalige Mitglieder der ebenfalls verbotenen „Heimattreuen Deutschen Jugend“, die für die AfD-Fraktion arbeiten sollen. Beide Organisationen stehen auf der Unvereinbarkeitsliste der Partei – daraus ergibt sich die Frage, wie konsequent diese Abgrenzung tatsächlich umgesetzt wird.

Eine Beraterin für den Ausstieg aus der rechtsextremen Szene erläutert, weshalb eine öffentliche Distanzierung allein noch keinen glaubwürdigen Bruch mit früheren Überzeugungen belegt. Entscheidend sei eine ernsthafte Auseinandersetzung mit der eigenen Vergangenheit. Nach Angaben der DW beantworteten weder der angefragte Parteivorstand noch die kontaktierten Abgeordneten und Mitarbeiter ihre Fragen zu diesen Verbindungen.

Warum lesen: Der Beitrag verbindet konkrete Recherchefälle mit wissenschaftlicher Einordnung und beleuchtet die Grenzen zwischen formaler Abgrenzung und politischer Praxis.

Français — Webtoon : l’Asfored et Kenaz s’associent pour former de nouveaux auteurs

ActuaLitté · 12 novembre 2026 · https://actualitte.com/article/135204/auteurs/webtoon-l-asfored-et-kenaz-s-associent-pour-former-de-nouveaux-auteurs

Comment former en France des auteurs de bandes dessinées pensées pour le smartphone ? L’Asfored-Edinovo et le studio sud-coréen Kenaz proposent une première réponse avec la Global Webtoon Academy, lancée le 28 septembre. Quatorze étudiants y travaillent pendant deux mois auprès de professionnels coréens. Au programme : la construction du récit, le découpage et la réalisation graphique, avec une attention particulière au rythme d’une lecture verticale plutôt qu’au passage d’une page à l’autre.

Une remise de prix est prévue le 18 novembre à Paris. Les lauréats doivent recevoir chacun une bourse d’environ 3 000 euros ; les organisateurs évoquent aussi des possibilités de publication sur des plateformes internationales, sans les présenter comme acquises. L’ambition dépasse donc l’apprentissage du dessin : il s’agit de relier la formation aux débouchés professionnels et de développer un pôle européen consacré au webtoon.

L’article situe cette initiative dans un paysage culturel où le format gagne en visibilité. Selon une étude du CNC publiée en octobre 2026 et citée par ActuaLitté, 39,1 % des Français déclarent connaître les webtoons ; parmi ces personnes, 66,3 % disent en lire. Ce partenariat illustre ainsi la rencontre entre les métiers français de l’édition et les pratiques de création numérique venues de Corée du Sud.

Pourquoi lire : Pour découvrir comment un format de lecture sur smartphone transforme la formation des auteurs et leurs perspectives de publication.

Vocabulaire utile

  • webtoon — bande dessinée numérique, généralement conçue pour une lecture par défilement vertical sur smartphone.
  • découpage — organisation d’un récit en scènes et en cases afin de guider la narration et son rythme.
  • formation continue — apprentissage permettant aux personnes déjà engagées dans la vie professionnelle d’acquérir ou d’actualiser des compétences.

日本語 — ステンドグラスお掃除&修復シム『Piece and Quiet: A Stained Glass Restoration』11月27日配信へ。「2655個」の破片をゆったり直す、たまには“妖精”の手も借りる

AUTOMATON · 2026-11-13 · 記事を読む

山奥の荒れた礼拝堂で、割れたステンドグラスを少しずつ元に戻す。個人開発者のVahov gamesが手がける『Piece and Quiet: A Stained Glass Restoration』は、そんな修復作業を楽しむシミュレーションゲームだ。Spaghetti Catが11月27日にPC（Steam）向けに配信する予定で、日本語表示にも対応予定。プレイヤーは合計2655片のガラスを集め、ブラシで汚れを落としたうえで、色や形、下絵を手がかりに6枚のステンドグラスを完成させていく。

作業を進めると、破片の位置を示したり、必要な破片を呼び寄せたりする魔法を習得できる。清掃や配置の自動化に加え、妖精に破片を運んでもらうことも可能だ。一方、礼拝堂に残された手書きのメモやステンドグラスには、あるガラス職人の過去を知る手がかりがある。時間制限や失敗のペナルティはなく、作業時間を測るモードでも締め切りに追われることはない。記事では、手作業の楽しさと負担を軽くする仕組みが具体的に紹介されている。

読む理由： 修復という珍しい題材を通じて、競争や失敗ではなく、落ち着いた作業を楽しませるゲームの設計を知ることができる。

語彙メモ

  • 修復（しゅうふく） — 傷んだ作品や建物などを直し、元の姿に近づけること。
  • 下絵（したえ） — 作品を仕上げる際に、形や配置の基準として使う準備段階の絵。
  • 自動化（じどうか） — 人が一つずつ操作していた作業を、仕組みに任せて進められるようにすること。

中文 — 大众在中国的新能源汽车销量猛增347%

RFI 中文 · 2026-11-12 · https://www.rfi.fr/cn/中国/20261112-大众在中国的新能源汽车销量猛增347

据法广援引北德广播电台及大众公布的信息，大众汽车品牌2026年10月在中国售出28,000辆新能源汽车，同比增长347%。报道指出，大众今年已在中国推出11款新能源汽车，另有两款即将上市，并提出在2027年进入中国新能源汽车品牌销量前十名的目标。

不过，单月新能源汽车销量的增长，并不意味着大众在中国的整体业务已经摆脱压力。报道列出的数据显示，大众今年前三季度在中国的汽车交付量同比下降约30%；比亚迪10月的新能源汽车销量则超过25万辆，仍明显领先。文章将德国车企面临的困境与中国本土品牌崛起、市场竞争加剧以及价格战联系起来，适合对照阅读“局部增长”与“整体下滑”这两种趋势。

为什么读： 这篇短文连接德国汽车业与中国新能源市场，有助于练习辨别财经新闻中的统计范围和同比变化。

词汇笔记

  • 新能源汽车 (xīn néngyuán qìchē) — 中国汽车产业的分类术语，通常包括纯电动汽车、插电式混合动力汽车和燃料电池汽车，并非所有混合动力汽车都属于这一类别。
  • 同比增长 (tóngbǐ zēngzhǎng) — 与上一年相同时间段相比的增长，用于减少季节差异对比较的影响。
  • 交付量 (jiāofù liàng) — 在一定时期内实际交给客户的产品数量，不一定等同于订单数量或产量。
