Online KL Shampoo approximates full-matrix AdaGrad

Tilde introduces Online KL Shampoo, an optimizer that uses KL-optimal Kronecker factors to retain gradient-correlation history without the quadratic state required by full-matrix AdaGrad. Its inverse-square-root preconditioner uses a scaled, coupled Newton–Schulz procedure with no stale updates. In tests on models from 200 million to 1 billion parameters, Tilde reports 1.45× Muon’s parameter efficiency while preserving 98% of its training throughput.

Optimization · Language-model training · Scaling

Inside the 2.8-trillion-parameter Kimi K3 architecture

Sebastian Raschka examines Kimi K3 as a scaled-up descendant of Kimi Linear. The design combines Kimi Delta Attention, multi-head latent attention, LatentMoE, attention-weighted residual connections, and NoPE in place of RoPE. Attention residuals are credited with consistent quality gains at an estimated 4% extra training cost and 2% extra inference cost.

Architecture · Mixture of experts · Attention

A direct weight edit repairs collapsed neuron labels

Goodfire describes an RL-trained model in which 94% of neuron labels began with the word “texts.” A direct weight edit reduced that behavior to 5%, reportedly with limited side effects. Supervised fine-tuning performed better, but required a newly curated dataset, suggesting that targeted editing may be a cheaper intervention to try before retraining.

CHARLIE resolves interruptions in pathogenic repeat expansions

CHARLIE analyzes targeted sequencing data to detect and position interruptions within expanded tandem repeats. The unsupervised method works across sequencing platforms and repeat motifs and resolves interruptions at the level of individual reads—useful for studying repeat expansions implicated in neurological and other genetic diseases.

Tandem repeats · Sequencing · Genetic disease

RIPPLE finds replicate-aware gradients in spatial transcriptomics

RIPPLE is an R package for directional, cell-type-anchored inference of gene-expression gradients. It fits per-sample Poisson generalized linear models with total-count offsets, combines evidence across biological replicates only when effect directions agree, and reports signed gradient scores with adjusted false-discovery rates.

Spatial transcriptomics · Statistics · Cell signaling
