NanoGPT speedrun falls to 76.3 seconds

A new NanoGPT Speedrun record improves runtime from 79.2 to 76.3 seconds. Its principal change is a lightweight Dynamically Composable MHA final layer, which recomputes a 112-token local window using the same queries and keys, mixes post-softmax per-head attention maps into a shared map, and applies dynamically generated scales to each head’s values.

A MUDD MLP and custom kernel accompany the design, which reportedly reduces the required training-step count by 7%.

De novo proteins appear to occupy an intermediate region of sequence space

An alignment-free study compares evolutionarily young de novo proteins, intergenic open reading frames, canonical proteins, and randomized sequences in flies and yeast. K-mer analysis suggests that de novo proteins contain non-random residue-order patterns and sit between intergenic ORFs and established proteins in sequence space.

Rare sequences connecting those regions are enriched for predicted transmembrane helices, raising the possibility that membrane association provides one route through the earliest stages of protein evolution.
