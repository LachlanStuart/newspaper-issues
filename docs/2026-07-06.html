Computational Biology, Protein Design & Drug Discovery

OpenDDE: open-source co-folding reproduction claims strong antibody–antigen docking performance

Diego del Alamo · protein modeling, antibodies, drug discovery, open source

OpenDDE is described as an Apache-licensed open-source reproduction of Isomorphic’s IsoDDE. It claims to approach IsoDDE and beat Protenix-v2 on antibody–antigen docking performance, making it relevant for antibody/antigen complex modeling and binder-discovery evaluation.

SCALE benchmarks scFv–antigen cofolding and exposes weak within-target ranking by confidence scores

Biology+AI Daily · antibodies, benchmark, protein folding, drug discovery

SCALE is a large benchmark for scFv–antigen complex folding built from 3,800 SAbDab-derived complexes and about 197,900 predictions from AlphaFold 2.3 Multimer, AlphaFold 3, Boltz-2, Chai-1, Pairmixer, and varied inference settings. The key finding is that near-native interfaces remain rare, and common confidence metrics often distinguish easy targets from hard ones better than they identify the best pose within a target ensemble.

Ab-CASLR evaluates strict OOD antigen-to-antibody retrieval with CDR-aware late interaction

Biology+AI Daily · antibodies, retrieval, protein language models, OOD evaluation

Ab-CASLR reframes antibody discovery as antigen-to-antibody retrieval under a strict antigen-cluster out-of-distribution split. It combines ESM-2 antigen embeddings, IgBert antibody embeddings, CDR-constrained slot attention, and late-interaction scoring. Reported diagnostics suggest that antibody-side CDR locality is doing much of the useful work, while antigen epitope grounding remains a bottleneck.

FastBindRank distills Boltz-2 for ultra-large HDAC11 virtual screening

Biology+AI Daily · drug discovery, virtual screening, distillation, Boltz-2

FastBindRank distills expensive Boltz-2 structure-based binding predictions into a lightweight Morgan-fingerprint MLP, then uses it to screen 122 million PubChem compounds for HDAC11. The workflow labels roughly 1% of PubChem with Boltz-2, ranks the full library, re-scores the top 100,000, filters for binding and drug-like properties, clusters for diversity, and reports two synthesized novel candidates with in vitro HDAC11 inhibition.

Interface-seeded RFdiffusion designs chemically and phosphorylation-controlled oligomers

Biology+AI Daily · protein design, synthetic biology, RFdiffusion, biological circuits

This work introduces an interface-seeded generative strategy for de novo functional oligomers: validated protein–protein interfaces are preserved while RFdiffusion samples symmetry-compatible protomers around them. Reported demonstrations include C3/C4 structures, ligand- and metal-triggered assemblies, reversible phosphorylation-controlled oligomerization, dual-gated redox/phosphorylation behavior, membrane-binding applications, and transcriptional activation in HEK293T cells.

Perturbation analysis tests whether molecular LLMs generalize off the training manifold

Biology+AI Daily · molecular AI, robustness, drug discovery, LLMs

A controlled molecular perturbation framework uses chemistry-valid graph edits to test robustness of molecular LLMs on QED regression, IUPAC-to-molecule generation, and molecule-to-IUPAC generation. The summary reports steep degradation even at small graph-edit distances, task-dependent sensitivity to atom versus bond edits, and partial robustness gains from nearest-neighbor in-context tuning.

SA-MTP adds structure-aware graphs and label attention for multifunctional therapeutic peptide annotation

Biology+AI Daily · therapeutic peptides, protein language models, graph neural networks

SA-MTP predicts multiple therapeutic peptide functions using probabilistic secondary-structure profiles, ESM-2 contact priors, graph attention, label-to-sequence cross-attention, FiLM modulation, and adaptive per-label thresholds. The item emphasizes improvements on imbalanced therapeutic-function labels and interpretability through structure-aware adjacency maps and residue attributions.

Structural Biology, Enzymes & Genomics

CryoROLE recovers continuous inter-domain rotations from focused-refinement cryo-EM poses

Biology+AI Daily · cryo-EM, structural biology, protein dynamics

CryoROLE is a lightweight computational tool for single-particle cryo-EM that computes per-particle relative orientations between rigid domains. It produces rotation landscapes for continuous inter-domain motions that may be missed by discrete classification, with validations summarized on human fatty acid synthase, INO80–hexasome, and ribosome datasets.

TCR-FramePose decomposes TCR–pMHC docking and CDR3 geometry in a local-frame representation

Biology+AI Daily · immunology, TCR, structural biology, cancer immunotherapy

TCR-FramePose represents TCR–pMHC pose as pMHC-referenced rigid bodies for the whole TCR variable domain, CDR3α, and CDR3β. The summary says it recovers class-associated and noncanonical docking modes, improves affinity-related geometric signals over conventional descriptors, and localizes key information to CDR3β orientation and offset.

Enerzyme trains electrostatics-aware neural potentials for enzyme-catalysis mechanisms

Biology+AI Daily · enzymes, neural potentials, computational chemistry, reaction mechanisms

Enerzyme and Enerzymette target reactive neural network potentials for enzyme QM-cluster mechanistic studies, demonstrated on SAM-dependent methyltransferases. Contributions include sub-1,000-configuration DFT labeling for enzyme-scale clusters, electrostatics-aware modular NNP design, charge supervision, dielectric alignment, and workflow automation for scans and NEB transition-state searches.

Evo2 likelihood is context-dependent as a bacterial fitness proxy

Biology+AI Daily · genomics, DNA language models, variant-effect prediction

A quantitative evaluation of Evo2 zero-shot likelihood scoring across five E. coli datasets finds that likelihood–fitness calibration varies sharply by context. Stronger calibration appears for plasmid TEM-1 under antibiotic selection and some structural variants from thermal evolution, while promoter MPRA and chromosomal essential-gene DMS show near-zero calibration.

GenPerturb links perturbation transcriptomes to cis-regulatory elements using pretrained genomic models

razoralign · gene regulation, Perturb-seq, genomic models, interpretability

GenPerturb uses pretrained sequence-to-expression models to connect perturbation-induced expression changes with candidate cis-regulatory elements. It prioritizes regulatory regions and transcription-factor motifs, computes perturbation-minus-control attribution tracks, and aims to enable sequence-level interpretation without matched chromatin data.

U-Pert models unbalanced perturbation dynamics for cell-fate design

single-cell, perturbation modeling, virtual cells

U-Pert is an unbalanced generative framework for learning perturbation-induced cell-state and cell-abundance dynamics from unpaired single-cell data. It couples transcriptomic transitions with abundance changes and supports inverse design for target gene-expression programs and cell-type compositions.

MARVIN uses a structured latent population model for generative cell phenotyping

single-cell, generative models, VAE

MARVIN is a semi-supervised variational autoencoder for cell phenotyping that represents cells as discrete populations with continuous intra-population variation through a Gaussian-mixture latent prior.

PLNFGL estimates multi-condition gene networks from single-cell RNA-seq

single-cell, gene networks, transcriptomics

PLNFGL, or Poisson Log-Normal Fused Graphical Lasso, estimates condition-specific gene networks from scRNA-seq data by addressing dropout and distribution mismatch and inferring multi-condition precision matrices.

Gene Intelligence tokenizes single cells, metadata, and expressed genes for masked pretraining

single-cell, foundation models, representation learning

Gene Intelligence encodes each cell as a token sequence with a cell token, metadata tokens, and expressed-gene tokens. It jointly predicts masked tokens and counts, with downstream uses including cell-type classification, doublet detection, and cell/gene/sample embeddings.

Français — Livres générés par IA : le rapport veut les exclure des aides, mais comment les détecter ?

ActuaLitté · 2026-07-03 · https://actualitte.com/article/132456/politique-publique/livres-generes-par-ia-le-rapport-veut-les-exclure-des-aides-mais-comment-les-detecter

L’article analyse un rapport parlementaire français consacré à l’intelligence artificielle et à la culture, avec un accent particulier sur le secteur du livre. Le texte recommande de mieux encadrer les ouvrages entièrement générés par IA : déclaration obligatoire de l’usage de ces outils, sanctions en cas de fausse déclaration, exclusion possible de certaines aides publiques et clarification du statut au regard du droit d’auteur.

ActuaLitté insiste surtout sur la difficulté pratique de cette doctrine : il n’existe pas aujourd’hui d’outil fiable permettant d’identifier automatiquement un contenu artificiel sans risque d’erreur. L’article distingue les productions entièrement automatisées des œuvres hybrides, où l’auteur humain intervient de manière significative, et évoque l’idée d’une déclaration graduée du niveau d’intervention de l’IA.

Le papier aborde aussi des questions très concrètes pour la filière : aides du Centre national du livre, ISBN, taux réduit de TVA, droit d’auteur et critères administratifs. Il montre que le principe politique semble assez clair, mais que son application dépendra de procédures capables de qualifier précisément la part de création humaine.

Pourquoi lire : Pour comprendre comment la France tente de réguler les livres générés par IA sans disposer encore d’outils de détection vraiment fiables.

Vocabulaire utile

  • obligation déclarative — règle imposant à un auteur ou éditeur de signaler officiellement certains usages, ici le recours à l’IA.
  • œuvre hybride — création mêlant intervention humaine et assistance ou génération par intelligence artificielle.
  • taux réduit de TVA — régime fiscal appliquant une taxe plus faible à certains biens, notamment les livres sous conditions.

語彙メモ

  • 機密情報（きみつじょうほう） — 社外に出してはいけない重要な内部情報。
  • 秘密保持契約（ひみつほじけいやく） — 業務で知った情報を外部に漏らさないと約束する契約。
  • 恒久的差止命令（こうきゅうてきさしとめめいれい） — 特定の行為を将来にわたって禁じる裁判所の命令。
