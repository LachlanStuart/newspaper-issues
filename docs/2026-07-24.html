EVEE variant predictions tested against real-world biological evidence

Goodfire reports validation work on EVEE, an interpretability-based system previously applied to 4.2 million genetic variants. Its predictions were checked against a national biobank, clinical records, and RNA sequencing. Six variants supported by mechanistic hypotheses and literature evidence were submitted for possible ClinVar reclassification.

AAMFM conditions antibody design on antigens and epitopes

AAMFM adapts ESM3 for antigen-specific antibody design, combining antibody sequence and structure with antigen geometry and epitope annotations. Its training includes antibody-domain pretraining, CDR-focused masking, and calibrated preference optimization based partly on predicted complex scores.

Reported gains come from SAbDab benchmarks and AlphaFold3-derived metrics; the available report does not describe experimental binding validation.

LLMol uses deterministic rewards for molecular generation

LLMol adapts a general-purpose language model to simplified SELFIES, then uses Group Relative Policy Optimization with deterministic RDKit rewards to target molecular properties and perform similarity-constrained optimization.

The results illustrate verifiable-reward training for generated molecules, though the benchmarks emphasize proxy properties such as logP and QED rather than biological activity or laboratory validation.

Today’s systems-biology tools emphasize interpretable connections among receptors, signaling pathways, transcription factors, and changing gene-regulatory programs.

IntraTalker connects receptors to transcription factors and genes

IntraTalker combines intracellular signaling with CrossTalkeR’s intercellular analysis. Its proposed “receptomes” link receptors through transcription factors to downstream target genes, enabling receptor-perturbation simulations and models of cell-state transitions.

Tangerine reconstructs changing regulatory networks

Tangerine is a Python framework for reconstructing and interactively exploring dynamic gene-regulatory networks from transcriptomic time series. It combines time-constrained metacell aggregation, regularized linear models, and non-parametric correlation, with views ranging from gene-module evolution to individual time-varying regulatory relationships.

scLEMBAS models context-dependent signaling counterfactuals

scLEMBAS uses a recurrent model constrained by prior signaling-network knowledge while separating protein activity into context-specific and residual components. The factorization is intended to support counterfactual predictions of transcription-factor activity across perturbations and cellular settings without discarding a mechanistic network interpretation.

Hobrac scales genome comparison with conserved-gene anchors

Hobrac is a reference-guided genome-comparison workflow that selects chromosome-level references from a curated GenBank-derived database. It anchors synteny plots on conserved BUSCO genes, reducing distant assembly comparisons from millions of nucleotide alignments to a few thousand robust loci.

Evidence & Research Assistance

GeneKnow separates biological evidence retrieval from generation

GeneKnow is designed to keep evidence-critical operations—retrieval, passage selection, provenance tracking, and bibliography construction—separate from constrained generative analysis. It supports both multi-paper discovery and close inspection of individual papers while preserving links from synthesized claims to source passages.

The architecture directly targets citation fabrication and unsupported biological summaries, making auditability part of the research workflow rather than an afterthought.
